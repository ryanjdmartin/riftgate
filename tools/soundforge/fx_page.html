Effects, second pass

A fresh page. Every effect the game plays, sketched again beside what ships, in the three registers the screen already has: the console's green, the iron of the player's machines, the violet of the other side, and the red where it is about to hurt. Nothing here is a shipped file.

Play on the phone's own speaker first, then once more in headphones. Notes by section and row, as ever: "console 3 new", "rift 2 keep the old one".

How to A/B. A dashed button is what ships now; a solid one is the sketch. In the four rounds at the top the two files are the same bars with the same moments, so tapping the other button mid-round keeps your place (turn that off at the foot). The single sounds start from their top each tap. Every file is written at the level its sound plays at in the game, so the mix between them is the real one.

0. Four rounds

The set in context first, over the music the moment would have under it. Each is the shipped sounds, then the sketches in the same places.

1A round of the skirmish
Over Hold The Line's power chords: two dice picked, seated, rolled and locked; the plan committed; autoguns and a kill; a laser; a rack and two kills; the foe's intent settling; a ghoul's claw blocked; a brute's haymaker landing on a Kestrel; the deflector up, a vent, the coil; the stand held.
2The map's turn and the handover
Over Night Shift: five footfalls (new: treads, treads, legs, wheels, a flyer), a foe into the line, the alarm twice, the dive, the cut, the Risen through a gate, the gate struck and the Horde through it, The Chorus through another, a boss revealed (new only), and the line broken.
3The three families
Over The Crossing: the Risen, then the Infernal Horde, then The Chorus, each arriving, striking, and striking hard. Now, all three are the same spawn, slash and slam.
4The iron
Over Hold The Line: the five weapon shapes (barrel, twin barrel, rack, blade, laser, plasma, the deflector dish, the coil), then each maker's machine struck, holding, and dying: Minerva, Kestrel, Wainwright & Sutler, Aphelion, the Arsenal. Now, every blow is by damage type and every plate is the same steel.

Listen for: whether the new set reads as one world, and whether anything in it is quieter than it should be on the speaker. Then go by section.

1. The console

CARE's reconstruction, the green lines. One grammar for every press: a key (a membrane key under glass, a damped tuk, no relay spring), a reply (a flat tone tuned to the music's E: G3, B3 or E4, so it sits in every track), and work (chatter). The die is a mechanism, not a button.

1A tap
The key and a short reply on B3. Now: a relay spring and a tone off the key.
2Back
The same key, the reply lower on G3 and a hair longer.
3Plot a route
The console works, the key, the reply on E4, and a second softer dot: a route has two ends.
4Die picked up
Lifted from its socket: the key, a small plate lift, a short high reply.
5Die seated
Seated back: no tone at all. A damped knock, a little body, the socket's click. The drop is the one press that should not chirp.
6The roll
The readout's own digit scramble: ticks at the flip rate, thinning, each with a flat tone off any scale; the console under load under it; then the brackets collapse shut (two knocks) and the number settles on G3. Now: chatter over crushed ticks.
7The lock
The corners snapping on: the key, a heavier key with the latch's casing, the reply on B3, a short hold hum on E3.
8Resolve
The plan committed: the console works, the lines lock in one by one (three keys), the heavy latch, the reply low and long on E3. Now: a swell under chatter and a thud.
9Intent (new)
The brackets collapsing onto a foe's intent as its number settles out of the scramble: a crushed tick, the console reading the other side, and a small knock. Silent now.

Kept as they are: the codex opening and closing, the pause menu. They already speak this grammar.

Listen for: whether the keys read as one console, whether the seated die is right with no tone, and whether the roll is the readout you watch.

2. The iron

The player's machines, nineties 3D in five makers' metals. A blow sounds by the weapon's shape, since the mount rule made the shape the thing on screen. A hit, a block and a kill sound in the metal of the machine struck. A footfall by silhouette.

By shape

1Barrel
A slug cannon, a railgun: one heavy report, the breech cycling after, the hit. Now, every kinetic line weapon is a burst of four.
2Twin barrel
Twin autoguns: three double-taps, the action clacking, the last pair landing.
3Rack
A missile rack: four launches, each an ignition tonk and a whoosh (a band opening, not a pitch), then four impacts close together. Now, indirect fire is one cannon shell.
4Blade
A chainsaw claw: a motor at one speed (no glide), the teeth a flutter on it, the load dipping where it bites, the rip through plate.
5Laser
The capacitor's crack, a flat beam on B2 for a tenth of a second, and the hit a burn rather than a thud: a pop and a sizzle on the plate.
6Plasma (new)
The Arsenal's energy, which runs hot: a whoomp with a slow front, a sub hum on E1, a hint of a vowel under it (the manual written as liturgy), crackle, and the hit a hot splash. Now the Arsenal's guns sound like everyone's laser.
7Dish: the deflector
The key, the field's hum snapping on at B1 with E2 over it, a crack as it seals, a short bed, then the field settles and a relay cuts it.
8Dish: the coil (new)
An overcharge coil, a buff: the bank charging as a buzz on E3 growing in loudness, never in pitch, ticking faster, then the discharge's crack and the key.

By metal: struck, holding, dying

9Now: one steel for all
What ships: the same plate under every machine.
10Minerva steel
Matte steel, the baseline: the plate as it is now, dull and firm.
11Kestrel chrome
Thin, glossy, higher partials that die faster, a bright front, less body: an airframe, not a hull.
12Wainwright iron
Lower, heavier, a touch more ring (still under a tenth of a second) and grit: old-West iron.
13Aphelion ceramic
A dead tok: high, very short, a dry click. Composite from the deep-space programme.
14Arsenal gunmetal
Dense and low, a sub hum on E1 under every strike (a little creepy), and the red glass ticking.

Footfalls by silhouette

15A hex on the map
Now, every machine stomps. Treads clatter, legs stomp (the shipped one), wheels grind on grit with the suspension knocking, a flyer passes.

Listen for: whether a shape is recognisable blind, whether the makers tell apart on the speaker (Kestrel against Wainwright is the pair most likely to), and whether the Arsenal's hum is creepy or just mud.

3. The rift

The other side, drawn flat and painterly against the player's 3D. Each family gets its own blow, heavy blow and arrival. The Risen are dry bone and grave-light. The Infernal Horde is fire, loud and telegraphed. The Chorus is geometry: the choir on a cluster, gated and crushed as if the feed cannot hold it, and it talks to the console's own ticks. Now, every foe is the same slash, slam and spawn.

1Now: slash, slam, spawn
What ships, for every family.
2The Risen
A ghoul's claw: a brittle crack, a rattle, little weight. The heavy blow: grave-light held cold (a flat high hum with a tremolo on its loudness, and static) for a third of a second, then the crack and the ground taking it. The arrival: bone gathering, the cold hum swelling, the boom, the rattle settling.
3The Infernal Horde
A fist of slag: the thud with a whoomp of fire on it, embers after. The firebolt, lobbed: the whoosh of it coming, then the fireball with a roar in it. The arrival: a furnace door, the roar behind it, the door's iron knocking twice, the boom, the crackle.
4The Chorus
A lash: a geometric whip, a crushed rip gated hard with its halves flipped, a cold impact, two crushed ticks. The murmur, a word that hurts: the choir on a cluster (a minor second and a tritone over E) gated at 28 a second and crushed, cut dead, the impact crushed, and the console flinching: two of its own ticks. The arrival: the cluster swelling under a stutter that quickens, the boom with a crushed tail.
5A boss revealed (new)
The feed tears (a hard cut of static), the band's red rules (two knocks), the picture's afterimages (three crushed ticks), the name resolving out of a scramble (chatter), the order typing in, a low E1 swell under it all. Then the family's theme takes over. Now the reveal plays the contact alarm.

Listen for: whether the three families tell apart with your eyes shut, whether the Chorus's stutter reads as the feed failing or as a glitch effect, and whether the Risen have enough weight to hurt.

4. The red

Where it is about to hurt. Tuned to E so they sit in any of the six tracks; the dive counts in on the console's key; defeat ends on the feed losing signal.

1Contact
The horn a power chord on E2 and B2, the klaxon the console's own tone on B4 twice, the thud bigger. Now the horn is E alone and the klaxon sits on A.
2The dive
No drive spinning down (that was a glide, the one left in the set). The feed's floor rises as a filter opening, the console arms with a key under each count-in beep on B3, the beeps closing in, the last long on E4, until the cut.
3The gate struck
The same explosion, the structure groaning on B1 with E1 under it, three knocks of a big plate. Now the groan sits between notes.
4Victory
The power chord on E, and CARE logging it: the console works, a key, and the all-clear as two flat tones, B3 up to E4.
5Defeat
The hum on E flickering out, the last thing to fall, then the reconstruction losing the signal: a crushed burst of static and two console ticks into silence.

Kept as they are: the cut, the vent, overheat, withdraw, a foe stepping into the line, and the shield breaking.

Listen for: whether the dive still builds without its spin-down, whether the two-tone all-clear is irony or parody, and whether the signal loss lands as the end of the feed.

What a pick means

A console pick lands as its recipe in the forge. A shape pick means the blow's sound is chosen by the component's shape rather than its damage type, with plasma for the Arsenal's energy. A metal pick means a hit, block and kill carry the struck machine's maker. A family pick means a foe's blow and arrival carry its faction, and the reveal gets a sound of its own. A footfall pick means the map plays the mover's silhouette. Each is a small hook in the game; none is written yet.
