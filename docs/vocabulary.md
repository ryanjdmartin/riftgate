## Component Kind

| **Weapon** | A component that deals damage: spent on a foe within its reach, it deals its number in damage, then its keywords act and its heat lands. One that deals none is equipment. | **Shield component** | Spend on self or an ally to add its power in shield, capped at that machine's shield maximum. | implemented |

| **Buff component** | Equipment spent on a machine (itself, an ally, or either, as the component says) that grants it a status, so many stacks deep, through the end of so many rounds: Empowered for damage, Armoured against damage, Primed to burn on hit. | **Order of operations** | Everything that changes a number goes through one pipeline, in one order. Sources: the chassis passive and each fitted component's passive (until refitted), skirmish rewards (until the battle ends), buffs and debuffs (until the skirmish ends, or until the end of a given round). First suppression, shortest-lived source to longest: a live effect that suppresses another by id or tag turns it off, and a suppressed effect suppresses nothing. Then the gate: an effect takes part only if it is live, its trigger fires now, its condition holds, and, for an ability, its component is not destroyed (a destroyed component keeps its stat bonuses and loses its abilities). Then adds, all sources summed; then multiplies, all sources multiplied; then floors and caps; then overrides, where the shortest-lived source wins and the latest granted breaks ties. In an attack, the number (with what fired heat or a target's missing hull adds) comes before the pipeline's adds and multiplies; Nothing is ever stored as a total: every read asks again, so an effect that is gone stops answering. Statuses stack: the same status granted again adds stacks instead of a second copy; an add is worth its value per stack and a multiply is applied once per stack; a status that ticks applies its per-stack tick at its owner's turn, then loses one stack, and is gone at zero. | implemented |

## Holes

Every entry whose `design.holes` is not empty. This is the list of things the mechanics do not do yet.

- **HP** (implemented): A machine's health is its hull; the engine's code may say either word.
- **Shield** (implemented): Rates and shield sizes are placeholders. No in-combat trickle; a passive may bring one back for one chassis.
- **Heat** (implemented): Heat does nothing below the cap. Consider effects at high heat short of overflow. Cooling numbers are placeholders; cooling must stay below a typical weapon's heat or heat never matters, and a vent component must be worth a whole turn (tuning).
