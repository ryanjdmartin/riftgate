## Rules

| Term | Codex | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Order of operations** | Everything that changes a number goes through one pipeline, in one order. Sources: the chassis passive and each fitted component's passive (until refitted), skirmish rewards (until the battle ends), buffs and debuffs (until the skirmish ends, or until the end of a given round). First suppression, shortest-lived source to longest: a live effect that suppresses another by id or tag turns it off, and a suppressed effect suppresses nothing. Then the gate: an effect takes part only if it is live, its trigger fires now, its condition holds, and, for an ability, its component is not destroyed (a destroyed component keeps its stat bonuses and loses its abilities). Then adds, all sources summed; then multiplies, all sources multiplied; then floors and caps; then overrides, where the shortest-lived source wins and the latest granted breaks ties. In an attack, the number (with what fired heat or a target's missing hull adds) comes before the pipeline's adds and multiplies; Nothing is ever stored as a total: every read asks again, so an effect that is gone stops answering. Statuses stack: the same status granted again adds stacks instead of a second copy; an add is worth its value per stack and a multiply is applied once per stack; a status that ticks applies its per-stack tick at its owner's turn, then loses one stack, and is gone at zero. | implemented |
