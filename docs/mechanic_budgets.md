# Mechanic budgets: a proposal

For the depth-and-breadth session and the batch evaluation of the proposed chassis, held as
one session (Ryan, 13 October: a session of its own measures the roster against itself for
mechanic depth and breadth; judging proposals without the roster-wide budget is how stuns
multiplied). Claude's proposal of 13 October; the numbers are placeholders for Ryan to move.
The one-to-four and solo measurements it leans on are in `docs/measurements.md`, 13 October.

## Four rules, the numbers placeholders

1. **Hard control is the scarcest class.** Never on a basic, at most one source in any
   chassis's locked kit, Numbed the systemic brake. A target of one source per eight
   components across the roster; today three in 54 plus one passive, two more proposed.
2. **A new chassis earns its place** by reading a state nobody reads, producing a state nobody
   produces, or changing what a kind of piece does for everyone. Another source of an existing
   state needs a reason beyond its maker's flavour.
3. **Prefer verbs that change the enemy's problem over verbs that remove its turn.** Soft
   control and positioning are the thinnest classes, two pieces in the roster.
4. **Every passive needs two routes in and two payoffs** from other makers' pieces, so it
   composes with the roster and not only with its own locked kit.

## The heat spectrum to field

A cool weapon is also soft cooling (Ryan, 13 October). Heat builds run from a kit tuned to its
passive cooling that never vents to a hot kit that buys its heat back with vent turns, the
middle being how many vents and how many cool weapons a build runs. The session fields one
chassis three ways and reads the heat row's signals (bible section 5): the worst case is a kit
with the hot build's damage that ends the fights that matter with heat budget unused. Today the
hot side is thin: the Sparrowhawk is the one machine that feels heat, so "a build that wants to
run hot" is a class to add on purpose.

## One question the session weighs

Cooldowns in a skirmish (bible section 8, reopened 13 October): a printed rule on a specific
piece, shown on the die as an entry blank for a round or two after it fires, paid in the die's
reliability; a second axis beside heat for pricing a big hit. Ryan is willing to be convinced
either way.

## Two measures the session adds

- **Solo value beside team value** (`tools/balance.gd --team`): a detachment-class machine beats
  a small pack for under 15% of its hull and loses a formation; a line machine the reverse. Today
  the cars cannot fight alone and the Honey Badger can.
- **Compositions at a fixed four** against formation-sized fights, since count dominates
  composition: a four of tank, controller, bruiser and support reads the same as a four of hitters.

## A first read of the proposals by these rules

Most novel: Minos, the boiler, the revolver, the multi-rotor, the gunship and the fan-boat, each
changing what pieces do or what the player controls. Most redundant: the funnel-web and Misharu,
two more stuns, and one of Mastema and the Onryō, two more damage-over-time machines. Aphelion's
proposal is read as a list of ideas in a vacuum (Ryan, 13 October: that session went off the
rails), not as a design to defend, and it carries no bench of ideas set aside.
