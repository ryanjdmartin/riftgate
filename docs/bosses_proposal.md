# The bosses: a proposal

Each domain needs three distinct bosses before its hero, drawn without repeats (the bible's
section 9, "Three bosses beaten"), so the game needs twelve; four exist. The 9 October pitch
never reached the records, so this file holds the slate until Ryan has marked all of it; then
its picks become content and a bible row, and the file retires (git keeps it). Names are
working names, and everything here is Ryan's to move.

## Open at the Peak

One slot if the Golem stays, two if it goes (the Summoner, below).

- **Fire Naga** (the first game's Marilith): six arms, six strikes at the hottest machine, each
  its own blow, so a shield stops one blade; asks for a lure onto a cold machine, the Peak's
  third answer. Runs today. A creature of the fire cave, never a demon: the demonic is the
  Arsenal's (the bible's section 1).
- **Behemoth**: Meteor at every machine as it dies (King Behemoth), so the kill is the
  telegraph: shield up, then kill it. A blow on death is a small engine piece beside the Gel's
  split; a Meteor below half runs today. The Nemesis's Meteor Artillery shares the word.
- **Tonberry**, a wildcard: a small lantern-bearer whose move counts down in its intent and
  stabs when it arrives, so the telegraph is the fight. Needs a countdown (the delayed blow,
  held longer). Its fit is the telegraph, not the fire.

## The Summoner and Ifrit (open)

Ryan, 15 October: if the reference is the genre's summoner, it calls Ifrit, not a Magma Golem.
Two forms:

- **The pair**: Ifrit joins the fight massive and stays, the Summoner in the back as the brain
  (the bible's hero-plus-beast pattern), in place of the Golem it calls today. Runs today.
- **The summon** (Claude's lean): the Summoner's move is Ifrit's attack at every machine, and
  Ifrit leaves after it; Pyroclasm becomes Ifrit's, since in the games the summon makes the
  attack. Every foe move is announced a turn ahead, so it runs on today's engine; the new work
  is Ifrit's art and a moment on screen for it to appear. It keeps the Summoner apart from the
  Tamer and the Necromancer, who call things into the fight every round, and its answer is the
  back rank: reach the Summoner, Suppress it or Disrupt it, or shield every machine.

The name is folklore's (a djinn of fire); the look is ours, drawn from the tradition and never
the series' horned design, and a spirit of fire rather than a demon (section 1). The
Summoner's circles stay deep violet: the fire is Ifrit's.

If the Summoner calls Ifrit, the Golem is no longer its identity, so the reason to replace it
as the Peak's boss goes. If it stays, it and the Tortoise are both slow lava heavies that erupt
below half: one of them changes its job (the Tortoise's could be its shell alone, the eruption
left to the Golem).

## Bench

The ideas set aside, each with what set it aside.

- Phoenix: it rises once from its ashes, the Drowned King's move and the Marsh's signature
  (Ryan, 15 October).
- Elder Drake: somewhat uninspired (Ryan, 15 October); it would also have been the third
  dragon, after the Drake and the Bone Wyvern.
- Ifrit as a boss: it is a summon, so it is the Summoner's (Ryan, 15 October).
- Bomb King: waits on the Bomb's verdict in play.
- Red Dragon: another dragon.
