Engagement Sandbox

The redesigned round: packs show where they walk; you fight one fight at a time, and a machine that fought is spent; packs join a fight when their ring covers the target; on their turn survivors walk on, round your machines, which never stop a pack. Hunters catch, and a caught machine fights first.

The decided rules, as the engine runs them: the enemy commits first, zones where a pack ends, a body in the path as the only stop, push, aggressive and hunt, and the Prairie Dog's provoke to judge. Place machines where they end their move, read where every pack goes, then resolve the phase and plan the next.
