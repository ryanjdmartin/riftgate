# The domains' statuses, and Phased by source

Ryan, 19 November: "I think yes let's work these buffs and debuffs into the domains more
prominently. Variety is what makes things interesting, especially when our foes have multiple
moves. It also gives Ward a reason to exist. Propose some additions or swaps to the current
lineup." And of purge, the same day: "Purge doesn't necessarily need to be in front of a blow.
It can ride on a debuff, or stand on its own." And of Phased: "Phased: Cannot be targeted,
damaged, or affected in any way by non-phased units", with the fallout he listed, "I would want
to expand it to more foes and ally mechanics, make it more core than niche", and "I would also
be curious about promoting it to a more accessible reactive defensive tool, removing the
end-of-round application delay".

Nothing proposed here is in content. Every row is a proposal for his marks; the numbers are placeholders
sized after his marks by the anchor read, and the shown text is drafted then. Each change is
written from the foe's fantasy, never from a table of who owns what, and a domain's palette is a
lean, never an exclusion.

## 4. Purge's three forms

His forms: ahead of a blow (the Waste's needles, maws and knife today), riding a debuff, or a
move of its own (the Djinn's Unmake). The read of 19 November put a purge ahead of a boss's
main blow at four to six points of a squad's hull, and the Barrier no pass. A purge that stands
alone takes the place of a blow, so it needs no compensation; one that rides a debuff adds to a
move that deals little. So the rows above use the other two forms, and a purge ahead of a main
blow stays the Waste's. Two calls of his were already open on the same question: whether the
Ruby Colossus's Burrowing Tentacle and the Lancer's Jump purge before they land.

## 5. Phased by source

### His rule, and what follows from it

"Cannot be targeted, damaged, or affected in any way by non-phased units." Built as he stated
it, each of these follows and is no question:

- A Phased unit's own buffs and debuffs act on it again: Ablating and Vitriol take their points,
  its Regenerating pays. A status already on it counts as its own.
- Its own heat burns it past its maximum, and its own entry's costs land: a Volatile bite, a
  corrupted entry's bite. Both were blocked by his rulings of 16 November, which this replaces.
- Its allies cannot buff, mend, cleanse or shield it, unless they are Phased too. That
  enforces his ruling of 15 November on both sides, so a ghost's own side stops reaching it.
- A Phased unit may still act on everyone. A Phased machine hits Phased foes, and a Phased foe
  hits every machine, Phased or not. "Bring a Phaser with you."
- A non-Phased foe's blow at every machine passes a Phased machine by; a Phased foe's lands on
  all of them.
- A fall's curse from a non-Phased foe cannot reach a Phased killer, so a Phased machine pulls
  up a Mandragora safely.
- A non-Phased ally cannot extend a standing Phased.

### The holes, where his words give two answers or none

1. **A status's source.** Whether a status already on the unit acts as the unit's own. His
   fallout says the damage-over-time ones do. I would read every status that way, so one check
   covers everything: an effect's source is the unit that lands it now.
2. **The battle's own effects.** Nothing in a fight today has no unit as its source. I would
   keep it so: any future effect names one.
3. **The guards.** The last foe standing never fades, and no foe fades two rounds running. I
   would keep both: a squad without a Phaser still needs a foe to end the fight on.

### As a system

Who each defence protects, against each shape of harm. "Squad" is what the squad as a whole
keeps; "self" is the machine itself.

| Harm | Armour | Barrier | Lure | Ward | Suppress | Phased |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| One big blow | a little, hull only | all of it | moves it to a better machine | none | all of it, a round | self all; squad none, the blow goes elsewhere |
| Many small blows | most of what reaches the hull | one of them | moves them | none | all of them, a round | self all; squad none |
| A blow at every machine | a little | one machine's share | none | none | all of it | self all; squad that share |
| A debuff or a purge at one | none | none | moves it | all of it | all of it | self all; squad none, it goes elsewhere |
| A damage-over-time laid before | none | none | none | none | none | none |

So Phased is personal safety, as his frame says, and a squad defence only against blows at
every machine. That is what keeps it from being a Barrier with no weakness: everything aimed at
one machine still lands on someone. Its threats: Phased foes, damage over time laid before, its
own heat, and being cut off from its allies' help. The counterplay on the foes' side is the
same list turned round.

### The reactive form

His design of 15 November put the delay there on purpose: "for the player it turns it into a
proactive tool instead of a reactive one", "with the caveat that you have to survive the
current round", "a safe reboot if you can pull it off". Granting it at once trades those for an
answer to what the intents show. What it raises:

1. **The blows already drawn on it.** Your frame says Phased "moves targets away", so they pick
   again, the next machine in line. The other answer is that they are lost, which would make it
   stronger than a Barrier.
2. **How long it lasts.** "Until the end of this round" covers the foes' turn for a machine
   that phases in the player's turn, but nothing for a foe, which phases in its own turn. "Until
   its next turn starts" works for both, and a ghost then plays as it does today.
3. **The reboot.** An ally's Phaser makes any machine's reboot safe in the turn it is needed.
   Today that takes a round's planning; the reboot's price is the bare turn.
4. **The purge window.** Today a purging foe can strip the wait before it lands. Nothing would
   replace it.
5. **The gate.** "All phased granters need to be gated, it should be earned." Whether the source
   rule balances it enough to relax that is his call.

My lean: build the source rule as he stated it, the three readings above with it; and put the
reactive form on a trial dial so he can play both, since the delay was his design and the
reboot is the rule most at stake.

### What it would need

- Foes that Phase: the Spectre and the Wraith today; the Firelord's Submerge above; and the
  tier-one Ghost, if the Marsh should meet it from the first battle on.
- Foes that reach a Phased machine: every Phased foe, by the rule.
- Pieces of ours: one granter, the Swallowtail's Mooring Screens, on a machine in design. A
  slottable Phaser at tier one or two, and a module, each proposed in chat first.

## 6. The bench: ideas a rule killed

- A fall that purges, such as a gel that strips the killer as it splits: a fall never purges
  (28 October), since it would hold through the whole enemy turn after.
- A fall that Exposes its killer: a fall lays no Expose, since an Expose is announced.
- Purge as a rider on a blow: a purge is a special, never a rider (28 October). His "ride on a
  debuff" is a step on the move, which the engine runs.
- Brittle 1 from a fall: Brittle halves, so its least is 2 (16 November), though a fall in the
  player's turn would still feel 1 through the foes' turn.
- Two Exposes in one round from two carriers: one mark at a time, so "every foe turns on it" has
  one answer.

## 8. Open from 20 November

- **A boss's three moves.** "A boss or hero three" is the move count he passed on 24 October
  ("The rest is fine"), and the content test holds it. King Gel's Dissolve would be a fourth. So
  either a boss may carry four where its kit wants one, or Dissolve rides a move it has.
- **A leader's numbers by tier.** He asked whether a boss's step numbers grow with its tier. They
  do not: a leader's HP grows by a tenth and a quarter, its attack by 1 and 2, and an armoured
  kit's plate by its step; a Purge 1 or a Sap 1 is 1 at every tier. My lean: content may write a
  step's number a tier, three numbers where it wants them, read as the leader is scaled.
- **Phased's granters.** His lean: "I would probably only allow self-phased except in very
  specific circumstances, like maybe the swallowtail." Self-phased answers the reactive form's
  third hole, since a machine that phases itself has spent its die and cannot reboot that turn;
  and it means the reactive form gives no safe reboot at all, where the delayed one gives the
  phased round to reboot in. His question: Phased is not the only thing on the round's end. That
  clock also ends Essence Flare and grants the Ammit's new one, runs a swallow's and a Golden
  Rage's rounds, and bursts the Web Bomb, and a part grows back after it. Phased is the only
  status another piece queues there.
- **The rally.** His: "It's a foe move, they're all different. Like if 'rally' always grants 2,
  it's a nuisance in tier 1 and laughable in tier 2 ... The point of the previous move pass that
  turned moves into steps was to decouple things like this." The formations that rally, for his
  read: the wolf, warg and alpha packs; the Captain's warband, the metal drift and the frost and
  bloom; the shaman's warband and the Leshy's hunt; Floraison's two hunts and the Mantis Ant's
  tier-three nest; the Fallen Weapon and its vigils; Gilgamesh and Enkidu; the magma forge and
  the caldera; and the Obsidian Tyrant's Saurian Queen.
- **The goblins' wall.** His "Give the goblins an aoe shield too". Four goblins each laying armour
  1 on every foe would stand the formation at armour 4, past the basic gun's 3. So either the wall
  refreshes rather than adds, armour 1 from walls however many raise one, or one goblin carries
  it and is the target.
- **The Wild Hunt.** Three riders, each naming the quarry, is the alpha pack's case in an elite.
