## Reach, greedy

What a spend of each reach did (lures and probes, which deal nothing, left out): its share of attack spends, the hull it took off foes a spend (every foe the blows reached, overkill not counted), the heat it paid a spend, the spends aimed at the back rank, and for indirect the shield its danger close cost per spend aimed at the front. Over every row and pack, then over the packs that field a back rank.
