## Reach, greedy

What a spend of each reach did (lures and probes, which deal nothing, left out): its share of attack spends, the HP it took off foes a spend (every foe the blows reached, overkill not counted), the heat it paid a spend, and the spends aimed at the back rank. Over every row and pack, then over the packs that field a back rank.

**Every pack**

| Reach | Share of attack spends | HP a spend | Heat a spend | Aimed at the back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| direct | 79% | 3.2 | 2.0 | 20% |
| indirect | 21% | 2.9 | 1.8 | 22% |

**Packs with a back rank**

| Reach | Share of attack spends | HP a spend | Heat a spend | Aimed at the back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| direct | 80% | 3.5 | 2.1 | 48% |
| indirect | 20% | 3.3 | 1.9 | 56% |

In the packs with a back rank, 50% of the damage the machines took (shield and hull) came from the back rank.
