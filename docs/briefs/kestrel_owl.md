# Brief: the Snowy Owl, Kestrel's stealth unit (new chassis, direction)

The session designs the tenth of Kestrel's ten, marked by Ryan on 21 October as a stealth
unit. The Snowy Owl is his name for it ("probably"); a plain Owl is a Heavy Gear machine (the
reserved list), so the species stays in the name. The look and the numbers are Ryan's.

**Status**: direction (Ryan, 21 October); the first round proposed the same day. **Maker**:
Kestrel. **Tier**: open (Kestrel's counts: 3-5-2 puts it at two, 4-4-2 may put it at one).
**Read first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`; `kestrel_line.md` (the line, the overlap map, the last
two slots); the bible's Phased row, the Guard sentence of the redirection row and the cars row;
`docs/rules.md` on the round and the foes' targets.

## What Ryan said

## What frames it

- No rule hides a machine today. Phased (the Swallowtail's, on main) cannot act, take damage
  or be targeted, so it is shelter, not stealth. On the map the Prairie Dog is never picked by
  a hunter (its passive), so "unseen on the map" is half taken.
- The round: every machine sheds its cooling, then every foe picks its move and its target,
  and the pick is a commitment that Lure and Disrupt move. A stealth read when the foes pick
  needs no re-pick mid-round.
- A standing draw made one machine the target of everything (Guard, built and taken out: the
  redirection row); a machine that is never a target is the same lock inverted, its blows
  landing on its team.
- Owls fly silent (comb-edged feathers); the snowy owl hunts by day over open ground, white.

## The first round (Claude, 21 October)

**A. Cold is quiet (Claude's lean): heat is its signature, and a cold Owl is unseen.**

- The passive (a working name, Silent Flight): no foe picks it while it carries no heat as the
  foes pick (after its cooling); a blow at every machine still finds it. Its kills are silent:
  a foe it kills gets no Last Act and does not get back up.
- Heat is its stealth meter: a gun whose heat its cooling clears keeps it hidden next round, a
  hot gun is a flare and it is seen next round, a vent hides it again. The decision every
  round: the big hot shot and be seen, or the quiet one and stay hidden; and the team's order,
  since its hiding sends the blows to the others.
- It reads its own heat (Kestrel's resource, inverted: the Buzzard wants it high, the Owl at
  zero), produces safety and kills that stick (the Marsh's revivers, a boss's Last Act), and
  pays in cold, smaller guns and vent turns.
- With basics alone it works (cold basics and the basic vent hide it), so it could be a tier
  one by the test; its new rule reads tier two.
- Holes:
  1. The blows it dodges land on its team. Priced by its cold guns and by the team's
     knowing it; the levers: a heat line above zero, or the stealth lasting only until it
     strikes.
  2. The silent kill is one more answer to the Marsh beside Suppress, Disrupt and kill order,
     and the planned Purge strips Getting Back Up too; against a Last Act it is a second answer
     beside the shield raised before the kill (the threat-shape row: two answers or more, none
     with all).
  3. A team of cold machines is hidden only on the Owl; the rule is the passive's alone.

**B. The hunter on the map: unseen until it strikes.**

- On the map packs never see it: no hunter catches it, and it may pass a pack's ring without
  opening a fight. A fight it opens begins with its ambush, a spend before the foes pick.
- Holes: the Prairie Dog's never-hunted rule is the same clause (parted by the ambush: the Dog
  is never caught, the Owl catches); a free spend before the picks is tempo; the four-machines
  row moves the squad as one, so the lone hunter is a detachment, priced by time.

**C. Hidden as a status in the skirmish.** A buff no foe can target, gained by its lock, lost
when it deals damage, its blow from hiding doubled: the classic loop, with more bookkeeping (a
re-pick when it hides mid-round) and the same hole as A's first.

A two-clause passive (the map half and the fight half, the Prairie Dog's and the Swallowtail's
shape) can join B to A or C.

## Since then (5 November)

Ryan, 5 November: the Magpie and the Snowy Owl are "completely unspecced other than general
directions and names". Claude's note the same day, from his chassis ideas of 4 November
(the briefs README's "Ten a maker"; the frost figure's brief, `git show 084500a7:docs/briefs/arsenal_frost.md`): of the ideas

hitter, so an owl that frosts its prey and strikes it stays cold and unseen. The Cryo detonator's
Suppress at enough stacks reads as the prey frozen in place. The Arsenal's frost figure could keep
the version that blows the frost up for damage; the two part by payoff, silence against the blow.
Ryan, 5 November: "assuming we take the cryo direction with the owl - which makes a lot of
sense". His lean, the kit still to design.
