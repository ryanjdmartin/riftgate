**Ryan's direction (11 November)**: "Swallowtail we can punt because that guy wasn't finished
anyway. Needs a skirmish niche properly carved. It's got solid base mechanics but nothing solid to
do with them." Punted: the Muster Deck (the carry and the Muster) stands, and its session carves
the niche first, the pieces after. Undealt meanwhile, so Wainwright & Sutler fields one tier three,
the Musk Ox, itself unfinished.

Wainwright & Sutler's first tier three, the helicarrier, built and fielded (the bible's
Swallowtail row, Ryan's go of 21 October: a mobile HQ, a Muster its locked pieces spend), its kit
never finalized: its design session is his to start (4 November), and its Reveille Horn is to be
replaced entirely (8 November: "a horn is kind of silly"). The refit pass takes two more of its
four locked pieces (`docs/briefs/refit_pass.md`), so three of four are now open. Ryan, 9
November: "Your swallowtail changes are probably correct, but that design was never finalized
anyway."

## Why the model breaks it

- The Mooring Screens are a shield on every other ally (charged: Phased on every ally) and the
  Sutler's Hoist a shield on an ally that cools it too (charged: its shield full, its heat gone,
  cleansed). Both are shield entries and the Hoist is a vent besides; neither kind stays on a die.
- The Screens were "the team shield, honest by its number", the form its row passed where
  Armoured on every ally and a Barrier on every ally were refused. With no shield number to hand
  out, that passing form is gone; his word of 10 November reopens a team Barrier or a team Ward
  for it (below).
- Its stock carries a Canopy, which retires.

## What stands

- The Muster Deck, both clauses: the carry on the map, and the Muster filled by every die that
  rolls and spent unasked by its charged pieces.
- The Spotting Flare (Expose; charged, Suppress too).
- Its body and model, and the fantasy in his words: "It doesn't rain death, but it directs
  units to rain death, offers in-fight refueling and support, and gives them a lift when needed."

## A team Ward or a team Barrier: his word, 10 November

The Screens as the team's Ward and the Hoist as an ally's Armoured were offered in chat on 9
November and he called them probably correct; Claude then withdrew the first against the Barrier
and Ward row ("the block is a buff, one machine at a time"). His answer, 10 November: "A team
ward is probably ok, as is a team barrier, really, it's just combining them in a way that doesn't
let both be cast back to back granting perpetual invulnerability. So yeah, I would consider the
swallowtail as a potential home since it has a lot of levers. But not necessarily a decision to
make now." So the withdrawal is undone: either may sit here, the two never chained into a
standing block, and this machine's levers (the Muster, a charged form, a cooldown) are what
would hold them apart. Nothing is decided; it is his session's.

## Bench, Claude's, for his session

- The Screens as the team's Ward (9 November's offer, open again on his word above); or a team
  Barrier as its charged form, the two on one piece so one die never casts both in a round.
- The Screens: every ally's shield regenerates double this round (a regen buff, the kind his flat
  regen keeps meaningful); charged, Phased on every ally as today.
- The Hoist: "in-fight refueling" as the model has it: a Cleanse on an ally plain; charged, the
  reset, the ally cleansed and a piece it has spent this round brought back.
- Cooling an ally: the Hoist does it today. The Puffin's rework makes that the Puffin's verb
  (landed 19 November, the bible's Puffin row: its pull takes an ally's heat onto itself); if
  the Hoist keeps a cooling, the Puffin is not the only one. His call across the two.

## The reboot's offer: the team's pit crew (Claude's, 17 November)

From the read of every machine under the reboot (`git show 302d6a4c:docs/reboot_machines_proposal.md`,
its Swallowtail section), for his marks: two of its four locks have dead plain forms, but the
reboot hands it the niche it lacked. In-fight refuelling is the reboot itself now, so the HQ can be
the team's pit crew: its charged Screens already give every ally the safe reboot (Phased), and its
charged Hoist, written to clear Broken and Overheated too, would be a reboot by another hand with no
bare turn, the dearest effect in the system, behind the Muster's gate.

## What the session hands back

Its three open pieces in chat, his marks, then content; the charged forms' prices; its runner
row; its model unchanged. Until then its two shield pieces are blanks once the pass's content
lands, so it is not dealt (those pieces marked planned).
