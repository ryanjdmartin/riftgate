The session settles the Baboon's move to tier two and what it stocks there.

**Status**: rework; Ryan's read (14 October: "the baboon to me reads as tier two, not tier 1,
maybe"); in session since 15 October (Claude's first round and its review, below; Ryan's calls
to come). **Maker**: Aphelion. **Proposed tier**: two (one today). **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 2 (the Baboon's rating); the bible's
stock-pass rows (section 7) and tier rows (section 10); `content/chassis.json` (baboon).

## Why tier two (Claude, 14 October)

Its Paradox meter needs statuses, and basics carry none, so at tier one it is not itself with
basics; it is a support frame, which the sort puts at tier two. At tier two it stocks tier-one
status pieces (the Differentiation Probe, the Polarity Projector, a shield). The cap that would
have kept the Umbra Inducer off it for good (no hard-control piece below tier two) was struck
(Ryan, 15 October), so the double-Inducer problem the analysis found (a stun gun filling the
meter for a second stun) is the session's to weigh, with the Inducer's tier, under the stun
row's rule: hard control always carefully considered, other options explored first.

## Holes already raised

- With the Baboon gone from tier one, Aphelion's tender deal is the Stingray every time until
  the line frame lands; the frame's session should come first, or the fixed deal is accepted
  for a while.

## The Aphelion session, first round (15 to 20 October)

Opened by Ryan on 15 October with the line frame, the jellyfish and the Carrier (its record is
in `aphelion_line_frame.md`): Claude's proposal in chat that day, Claude's review of it on 16
October at Ryan's ask, written down on 20 October. Ryan's calls of 20 October on the session
(every blow counts for the line frame, Purge for both sides, the battlecruiser never joining)
leave the Baboon's open; everything here is Claude's, for him to move.

**The proposal** (15 October)

- Tier two: yes.
- Its stock at tier two (pieces of tiers zero and one): the Halo Shield back; one status piece
  for each kind of debuff the meter counts (the Polarity Projector's Shock, a Burn piece); a
  basic weapon and a vent; no Umbra Inducer. The Inducer stays a tier-one blueprint the player
  finds, and two Inducers feeding a second stun becomes a build the player chooses rather than
  the default (Ryan, 12 October: no hard guard against a build that breaks the game); the runner
  reads a Baboon with two fitted Inducers against the bosses.
- The Paradox's threshold stays three.

**What the review corrected** (16 October)

- The tier-one test was overstated on 15 October. Its locked Flare does show the meter with
  basics alone, but slowly on a d7 (three Flares on one foe), so the case for tier two is the
  sort (a support, only good together), which is Claude's and partly agreed, not a test it
  fails.
- The move lands only with another Aphelion tier one beside the Stingray (the hole below).

**Since then, on main** (to 20 October)

- The stun row (section 5, Ryan, 17 and 18 October): price, never scarcity, and a Suppress is
  gated by a condition, a setup, a self-sacrifice or its timing, the Paradox the model; the row
  names "the Umbra Inducer ungated and the Baboon's session's". So leaving the Inducer a found
  blueprint is not enough: its gate is this session's question. Claude's lean in that row, for
  the stock pass: the Inducer suppresses a foe carrying a debuff (the Susceptibility Tensor's
  read). Its hole on this machine: Paradox counts as a debuff for that read (`docs/rules.md`),
  so on the Baboon's own targets the gate is nearly always open and the double stun stands.
- Ryan, 19 October: Aphelion needs a defensive tier one (the to-do's line for this session);
  with the Baboon gone up, its tier ones as proposed (the Stingray, the line frame) hold no
  body.
- Ryan, 22 October (the stock pass's stun calls, the bible's stun row): no Suppress piece sits
  in any chassis's stock, the Baboon's least of all ("no reason for it to be able to stock a
  suppress that powers a suppress, that should be a build choice the player has to make"), and
  the Umbra Inducer leaves Aphelion for the Arsenal, tier three, priced in Volatile with no
  condition. The proposal's "no Umbra Inducer" stands by rule, item 3 below is closed, and two
  Inducers on a Baboon is a tier-three find twice over, the player's build.
