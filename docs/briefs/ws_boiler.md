# Brief: the boiler (proposal)

The session finishes the eight-legged spider whose vents blast a random foe for the heat they
shed, and settles its tier.

**Status**: proposal with a place (the analysis, 14 October). **Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler.
**Proposed tier**: two in its proposal; one in the reshuffle (not decided). **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 4 (its rating); the bible's heat
rows (section 5) and its row on a damage rider needing a limit (section 2).

## The design (the proposal, with Ryan's calls of 12 and 13 October)

Its vents blast a random foe for the heat they take off it; cooling -10, so it gains heat every
round (Ryan, 12 October). The heat is its own (Ryan, 13 October): a vent it spends on an ally
cools the ally and blasts nothing, and an ally's vent on it blasts nothing (letting it blast is
the first buff if the boiler proves weak). The random target is the rider's limit, and the
player times it (Ryan, 13 October). Locked: the Steam Cannon. Four hardpoints.

The rest of the proposal (12 and 13 October; Claude's unless marked Ryan's):

- **The shown text**: "Its vents blast a random foe for the heat they take off it." The rule it
  bends: equipment touches only machines, and cooling never goes below zero. At cooling -10 it
  gains 10 heat at the start of every round, the boiler building pressure on its own (Ryan, 12
  October).
- **Why the heat is its own** (Ryan, 13 October): an ally needing a vent is a decision of its
  own, and venting a teammate's heat into a foe is thematically odd. An ally's vent on the
  boiler blasting nothing keeps the pressure its own problem, so no spare vent on the team
  turns into a free attack. The first buff, if it proves weak, would let an ally's vent blast,
  the Puffin's shield too (it sheds 10 heat).
- **The random target** (Ryan, 13 October): a crazy steampunk machine venting everywhere. The
  player times the chance: vent when only decent targets stand, as an opener while every target
  is alike, or at a lone boss, where random is no chance at all. It is the limit the rider
  needs, since the boiler vents every turn and so blasts every turn (the bible's row on a
  damage rider, section 2).
- **What it opens**: heat is ammunition and every vent is a gun. Routes to heat: the boiler
  itself, hot weapons of any maker, and the foes' heat debuffs: Burning adds its heat at the
  end of the player's turn, so it waits for the next turn's vents, and Scorched runs every
  weapon it fires hotter. Against the Peak it is paid for shedding heat it must shed anyway.
  Payoff: any vent of any maker; the Entropy Condenser, a vent of 40 for 10 hull, is its
  biggest shot.
- **Balance**: the gain must stay under a vent's number. At -10 a vent of 20 or 30 still wants
  a hot weapon behind it; at -20 or more a die of nothing but vents fires a free blow every
  round. The overheat lands at the end of the player's turn, so the boiler vents or burns every
  turn: the pressure is the play. Levers past the random target: the gain, or the blast dealing
  half the heat.
- **Engine**: the validator refuses negative cooling and the pipeline floors every stat at
  zero; both bend for it (the cooling step already adds heat when cooling is negative). A vent
  it spends on itself gains a rider: the heat it removed, as one blow on a random live foe,
  drawn from the skirmish's seeded stream. The forecast shows the blast's number and marks
  every foe it may land on.
- **Numbers and kit** (the proposal's placeholders, set under the whole-block shield rule):
  heavy, walker, move 20; hull 150, shield 30, armour 10, heat 100, cooling -10, field
  recharge 15. Locked: the Steam Cannon (a working name), Direct, flat 30, heat 30. Four
  hardpoints (a d5). Its stock, written for tier two with today's pieces as stand-ins: two
  vents and two guns (the Coolant Jacket, a basic vent; the Suppression Battery, whose heat of
  20 feeds the vents; the Maxigun). The stock pass rewrites every stock, and a tier-one chassis
  stocks basics beside its fixed pieces.
- **Set aside along the way**: a target the player chooses (12 October; Ryan: random, which he
  times and the passive needs); a vent spent on an ally blasting with that ally's heat (13
  October; Ryan: an ally's vent is its own decision, and thematically odd).

## The analysis (14 October)

A place. Vents as weapons is a product nothing has; a third reader of its own heat, with a
payoff of its own; the roster's first build that wants to run hot outside Kestrel, in the maker
whose kit is cold, which the heat row asked for. Every maker's vent is a route in and every hot
weapon fuel; the Peak's heat debuffs are free fuel.

## Its tier

The reshuffle (Claude, 14 October) proposes tier one: a basic vent already blasts, so it is
itself at once, and it is the heavy body Wainwright & Sutler's tender deal lacks. The hole:
negative cooling in a new player's hands from the first fight, which the Sparrowhawk's doubled
heat already asks.
