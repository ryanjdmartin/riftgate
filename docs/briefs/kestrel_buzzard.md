# The Buzzard: a rework

<!-- hangar: {"kind": "chassis", "maker": "kestrel", "tier": 2, "status": "direction", "slot": false, "name": "Buzzard", "built": "buzzard"} -->

**Ryan's direction (11 November)**: "I think the Buzzard can take the zap, but what do we do with
that sweet cannon on the model?" The zap is the Storm Petrel's, homeless since its rework
(`kestrel_storm_petrel.md`), in his form of the same day: "spend X shield and add it to the
attack". With it the Buzzard gives up firing all its heat as one blow, which becomes the Whip
Scorpion's shape alone (his first thought for it: a passed turn releases its heat,
`ws_whip_scorpion.md`).

Kestrel's tier-two medium flyer, built and dealt, one of the original designs with no review pass
(the bible's Buzzard row: "splits sustain from burst"; its fantasy, the Kite's larger cousin that
"trades explosiveness for longevity"). Today: its Thermal Recoupler adds 1 to its shield regen for
every 4 heat it carries (regen proper since 11 November), and its locked Afterburner Cannon fires
its whole heat as one blow on one foe (2, and 1 more for every 2 heat), leaving it cold.

## Why it moves

- Regen was its own when regen was rare (his word); every chassis regenerates since the refit.
- The Storm Petrel's hook, shield as it gains heat, is the same idea on Kestrel's tank.
- The Scorpion's release is the Afterburner's pattern on another trigger, his worry.
- Damage on one foe at tier two is the role map's most crowded cell, and it has no body.

## The direction

A chain, heat into shield into damage: its heat raises its regen (as today), and its shield is
spent into its blow. Each turn the player keeps the shield, longevity, or burns it into the shot,
explosiveness: the row's split as a choice rather than a roll, on a thin hull, Kestrel's risk.
The three part by what they do with heat: the Storm Petrel gains shield from it and keeps it; the
Buzzard turns it into shield and spends the shield on one foe; the Scorpion holds it and releases
it across many.

## The cannon (Claude's, for him to move)

The Afterburner Cannon stays where the model has it and becomes the zap's home: the engines fire
forward, fed by the field instead of the airframe's heat. Two forms:

- **A price**: it prints a shield cost (the engine runs one: short of it, the entry is locked) and
  adds what it spent to its blow. A known number, the same shot every time it can pay.
- **The whole field**: it fires all the shield it holds, a point of damage for every so many, as it
  fired all its heat. The bigger gamble, and the one that keeps the cannon's old line ("the hotter
  the airframe, the bigger the shot") with shield in heat's place.

Claude's lean: the whole field, on the cannon alone, so the zap is its signature and its other
guns stay ordinary; a module may borrow the priced form (his word, of modules borrowing a passive).

## His second thought (11 November)

"I wonder if the passive should still keep a flat shield cost for a zap, either as a base damage
increase or as a rider on hit. Or, it spends shield to add something like Chain. The choice could
be when to keep sustained DPS, and when to burst. That might give it too much flexibility but if
it's paying in safety, probably well earned." So two spends on one pool: the passive's flat cost
on its weapons, the sustained line, and the cannon's whole field, the burst; its heat refills the
pool through its regen. Holes raised in chat (Claude's):

- Short of the cost, the weapon fires plain: a printed shield cost locks an entry that cannot pay,
  and a passive that locked every gun on an empty field would leave it nothing to do.
- Paid whenever it can, or chosen: paid unasked (as the Nemesis's rage is) every shot thins the
  field the cannon wants, so the choice lives in what it fires and when; chosen a shot wants a
  control the die has not got.
- Damage or Chain, one: flat damage works with any gun; Chain lands only on Shocked foes, so it
  asks the team for Shock and gives the Buzzard reach on many foes where its tier lacks it.
  Claude's lean: damage on the passive, Chain a branch of its upgrades.
- With every weapon paying shield it is the old Storm Petrel's rule again, beside heat instead of
  in its place.

## Settled (Ryan, 11 November)

"Yes, when it can't pay it doesn't zap, same as the old Petrel. It's taking over the shield payer
identity while the petrel now goes more defensive. Automatically paid. Everything should be
automatic in this game, I don't want extra controls." And of Chain: "My thought about chain over
flat damage was just to offer another shock/chain chassis. But that might be overloaded."

So: its passive pays a flat shield cost on each weapon it fires, unasked, for flat damage, and
fires plain when the field is short; its cannon fires the whole field; its heat raises its regen.
Chain stays a candidate for an upgrade branch, his worry noted (overloaded). Numbers and the
cannon's rate are its session's, with the Storm Petrel's, after the heat pass.

## Holes

- The old Storm Petrel burned shield through every weapon; the Buzzard inherits the idea as the
  Petrel leaves it, on one piece rather than all.
- Empty, it has nothing between a blow and a hull of 28, and its regen is slow when cold: firing
  the cannon cold is the mistake the design should let the player make.
- The Puffin's heat dump (allies only) feeds its regen: a pair to read.

## What the session hands back

The cannon's form and numbers with the Storm Petrel's, after the heat pass; its runner row.
