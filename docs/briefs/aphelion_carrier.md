# Brief: the Carrier, the Protoss Carrier homage (proposal, open)

The session finds the Carrier's verb, designed beside Minerva's battlecruiser, the foil Ryan
framed it against.

**Status**: proposal, open (unfinished). **Maker**: Aphelion. **Proposed tier**: three. **Read
first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`; `minerva_siege_battery.md`; the bible's shield rule and
Barrier rows (section 5) and its rows on Aphelion's and Kestrel's next chassis (section 7).

## What stands

From the Aphelion chat (11 to 13 October), written up on 13 October for the batch evaluation.
The bible's Aphelion row records Ryan's call of 13 October: that session went off the rails,
and its proposal is "a list of ideas, not a design to defend". Everything here is Claude's
unless marked Ryan's, and the numbers are the proposal's placeholders.

- Ryan, 11 October, his brief for Aphelion: "a flying fortress (many options from helicarrier
  to capital-class spacecraft)".
- Ryan, 12 October: "What if the capital ship was the Protoss Carrier foil to the Minervan
  Terran Battlecruiser?" The two are best designed together; the battlecruiser does not exist
  yet.
- The frame: tier three, assault, a flyer, move 20 (round one).
- The proposal's round one, the flying fortress: a deep-space hull (the makers table's
  "deep-space programme"), the helicarrier and the capital ship at once, its lift fans gone
  with Ryan's propulsion call ("Aphelion doesn't use rotors as a general rule", 12 October). Three
  verbs, all new code: the deck (allies that end their turn
  beside it regain shield as if they had not fought); the long guns, joining fights from
  farther off (Ryan liked them and parked them while the engagement range may change); a second
  die for its air wing, the purest carrier and the dearest (two dice on one machine, on a phone
  screen).
- The proposal's round two: a locked Launch Bay giving every machine in the fight 10 shield (a
  screen), or interceptors as extra strikes on its weapons (many small blows, the target's
  armour coming off each one); interceptors that build up through a fight were ruled out when
  proposed and reopened by the bible's revision of the locked-pieces row (growth through a
  fight is fine for now).
- Assault is the largest size class, so a capital ship draws no bigger than any assault machine
  unless it is allowed a bigger draw.

## Since then

- The screen was proposed under the whole-block rule, which read it as one blow stopped on
  each machine (a standing shield blocked a whole blow however small; a swarm's second blow
  landed, and a breach went through). The honest shield rule (14 October) makes a screen of
  shield to every machine a fair number rather than four blows negated: 10 hit points on each
  machine, which a breach ignores. A Barrier is never granted to every ally at once, so a
  screen must stay a shield number.
- Ryan's taste, as the briefs README records it from his words in the Armadillo's go: no
  team-wide armour, Braced or shield handed out in bulk ("giving the team tank stats is just
  as toxic as taking all the blows"). Does a screen of shield to every machine pass that line?
  It stays open with the verb.
- Interceptors as extra strikes, the target's armour coming off each, are now the strafer's
  verb: Kestrel's tier one, decided 14 October, every weapon it fires striking twice at half,
  each strike losing the foe's armour. Niches may overlap, but mechanics should not repeat (the
  briefs README); the siege battery's brief flags the same overlap for itself.
