# Brief: the Carrier, the Protoss Carrier homage (proposal, open)

The session finds the Carrier's verb, designed beside Minerva's battlecruiser, the foil Ryan
framed it against.

**Status**: proposal, open (unfinished). **Maker**: Aphelion. **Proposed tier**: three. **Read
first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/aphelion_chassis_proposal.md` section 4.3;
`minerva_siege_battery.md`; the bible's shield rule and Barrier rows (section 5).

## What stands

- Ryan, 12 October: "What if the capital ship was the Protoss Carrier foil to the Minervan
  Terran Battlecruiser?" The two are best designed together; the battlecruiser does not exist
  yet.
- The proposal's round two: a locked Launch Bay giving every machine in the fight 10 shield (a
  screen), or interceptors as extra strikes on its weapons; interceptors that build up through
  a fight were ruled out when proposed and reopened by the bible's revision of the
  locked-pieces row (growth through a fight is fine for now).
- Assault is the largest size class, so a capital ship draws no bigger than any assault machine
  unless it is allowed a bigger draw.

## Since then

- The honest shield rule (14 October) makes a screen of shield to every machine a fair number
  rather than four blows negated, and a Barrier is never granted to every ally at once, so a
  screen must stay a shield number.
