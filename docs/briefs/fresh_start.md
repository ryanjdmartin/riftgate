# Brief: the pacing session and the foes' fresh start, one build

The design's one home while its session runs (30 October). Ryan opened the pacing session on
29 October ("I want to work on rift pressure, fracturing and rupturing, and by extension enemy
spawn rates and initial spawns"), settled its shape over the day, and on 30 October pulled the
foes' fresh start into the same build ("It has come to my attention that the foe fresh start was
never actually written, it was waiting on marks that I thought were settled. Since its extremely
relevant to this discussion, I want you to pull it in and make it part of this build. Many of
the open questions in it we have already discussed and settled here"). So this brief holds the
decisions of 29 and 30 October, the state of `docs/foes_proposal.md`'s fourteen questions, the
engine's shape, the build order and what measures it. It retires once its decisions are the
bible's rows, `docs/rules.md` and content (git keeps it). Every number is a placeholder.

**Read first**: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/rules.md` (the battle and the campaign), the bible's first
row, its section 4 (the round and pacing rows) and section 9 (the tiers, boss and ladder rows),

## The decisions

Ryan's calls carry his name and the date; the rest is Claude's lean he agreed to ("your reads on
everything seem great", 30 October), built as agreed.

1. **The three clocks, and what each prices.** Rift Pressure is time, the Rupture meter is
   progress, the gate is neglect. The formations stand on the field at the start as the
   meter's fuel; a peak sends a composition from the rift and patrols from the tears; the elites
   arrive at the Fractures. Every turn asks progress, defence or the curveball.
2. **A stall is priced, never free** (Ryan, 30 October: "I don't want a 2-hour battle where I
   take no damage and get crazy amounts of farm to be a legitimate way to play"). The flood
   bounds a stall's length but feeds the farm until the break, so three prices that exist or
   are decided: the gate through the tears (a pack landing on the apron strikes that turn, so a
   team holding the gate pays the fee whatever its shields), the yield by menace (salvage and
   the meter weighted by danger, the reinforcements light), and the flood's break. A design
   test: the flood breaks any team at about the same turn whatever its kit, read on the
   thickest fours; a fought battle meets one or two tears, a stall five.
3. **The tier is the kind, the ramp is the count and the speed.** The tier is locked when the
   rift opens and shown on the card (Ryan's form, the curve proposal): who comes, the
   formations' and patrols' sizes, Fractures a battle (three, four, five), how many elites and
   which, the boss's numbers and escort count. The ramp is the live Convergence, the same for
   every open front, never shown, monotone and never reset at a tier step (with the tier
   locked, a reset would make an old rift easier by waiting): the compositions on the field at
   the start and their density, the timer's fill, the tears a peak, the seam's creep. On
   neither: the thresholds (fractions of the field's menace at the start, so they follow both
   without a table) and the interval step inside a battle, on the table at zero until the
   turtle read asks for it (Ryan, 30 October: the escalation rate "should be on the table" as a
   ramp entity, since a team that specs for it could stall better).
4. **The flattening is the clock's picture** (Ryan, 29 October: art only, no rule on a rift
   tile). The seam creeps south a row or two a peak; no rule reads the flattened hexes; a loss
   when it reaches the gate is a round cap by another name and refused; a forced boss is
   refused (the boss's fight takes every machine and nobody leaves, and there is no battle
   withdrawal to retreat into).
5. **Tears** (Ryan's name, 30 October; micro-rifts in the chat). The battle starts with the
   rift alone. At a peak the rift sends its composition of the cycle, every armed tear sends a
   patrol, the seam creeps, and the ramp's count of new tears open on the seam's row, spread
   across the width away from the other mouths, each armed at the next peak (a tear opens a
   peak before it first sends, so where the next flood comes from is known an interval ahead;
   Ryan, 30 October: "do you mean the tear appears before spawning a unit?", taken). Tears stay
   open and never close. The rift keeps the formations, the tears the skirmishers (Ryan: "The
   most threatening formations can spawn from the main, large rift"). A spawn lands at the end
   of the enemy's turn on the nearest free hex not adjacent to a machine, so the player sees it
   and its walk for a whole turn and nothing appears on top of a detachment. One clock, more
   mouths as the ramp rises.
6. **The wall is struck anywhere** (Ryan, 30 October: "I agree about making the whole wall a
   target"). A pusher ending its walk beside any hex of the bottom rows strikes the gate's bar;
   the gate stays the look and the bar's name; the strike's floor of ten goes, so a stray pair
   chips less than a trio (Ryan: "a reduction to the minimum strike to make it more fair").
   City defences, when built, stand along the wall.
7. **Nine wide, the width parked** (Ryan, 30 October: a wider map needs horizontal scroll, which
   he was never against while the map-edge tags work; "it can be parked for now, might make a
   good finale fight at the Capital's gate"). A finale lever: the capital's field wider, scrolled.
8. **The pacing row's targets stand**: four to five meaningful fights then the boss, the count
   flat across tiers and the threat rising; the Rupture at three, four or five Fractures by the
   tier; later Fractures cost more.

## The engine

- **Compositions** (`content/factions.json`, `sim/content_db.gd`): a domain's `compositions`,
  each `{id, size, tiers, members}`, the sizes `patrol`, `formation`, `elite`, `escort`; a
  composition is dealt at a tier its `tiers` names, and a tier with none of a size takes the
  highest tier's under it. `spawn_table(faction, tier)` is derived, the tier's formations' and
  patrols' members merged, so the runners and the harness keep working; `escort_units` draws
  from it. `menace(foe_id)` is the heaviest implemented blow (damage times strikes, a breach one
  more) plus a quarter of HP rounded up, an elite's doubled. A foe flagged `elite` leads an
  elite composition; the validator refuses a hero in a composition, an elite composition without
  an elite, a size outside the four, an empty members list.
- **A pack** (`sim/unit.gd`): `members` as `[[foe, strength], ...]`; `foe_id` the lead's,
  `strength` the total; `foes_for` unpacks every member; dressed by the lead's behaviour, the
  slowest pace, the largest ring, winged only when every member is; the skirmish keeps survivors
  by type and the map keeps the members left; kills by member carry menace. The boss unit keeps
  its lineup and escort, its escort as members when a hand-tailored composition names it.
- **The ramp** (`sim/ramp.gd`): one table by the Convergence (0 to 12), placeholders:

  | Lever | Convergence 0 | 12 | Form |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Formations at the start | 1 | 3 | 1 + (C + 2) / 5, the second the guest's from tier two |
  | Patrols at the start | 2 | 1 | 2 for C under 8, else 1 |
  | Density (each member's strength scaled, at least one) | 1.0 | 1.5 | 1 + C / 24 |
  | The timer | 12 | 8 | 12 - C / 3 |
  | Tears a peak | 1 | 2 | 1 + C / 8 |
  | The seam's creep a peak, in rows | 2 | 2 | flat |
  | The interval step a peak | 0 | 0 | flat, on the table |

  By the tier (locked): Fractures 3, 4, 5; elites at the Fractures [1], [1, 3], [1, 2, 4]; the
  boss's numbers and escort count (ContentDB.BOSS_TIERS); the compositions' sizes are
  content's by their tier bands.
- **The field at the start** (`sim/battle.gd`): the formations on rows three to five spread
  across the width, the patrols on the flanks a row or two below, the boss a row below the rift
  with its escort, the seam at its start row; the cycle holds the tier's formations and patrols
  alternating, the lead's and the guest's alternating from tier two; the tears' cycle its
  patrols.
- **A peak** (`sim/battlefield.gd`): the rift sends the cycle's next composition; every armed
  tear sends the patrol cycle's next; the seam creeps; the ramp's count of tears open on the
  seam's row, each on a free land hex far from the other mouths, armed from the next peak. A
  spawn lands on the nearest free hex to its mouth with no machine adjacent. The timer and the
  rest are the ramp's; the interval step shortens the next peak by the table's step (zero).
- **The wall**: `on_apron` reads every bottom-row structure; the strike is a third of the
  strength times ten with no floor but one.
- **The Rupture meter** (`sim/battle.gd`): the tally of menace per kill, live; the thresholds
  set at setup from the field's menace (the packs placed, the boss unit aside), the last at two
  thirds of it and the steps growing (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3); a Fracture pays out as a fight ends:
  the log names it and the next elite composition of the lead's domain not yet fought this
  battle spawns at the rift at the Fractures the tier names; the last Fracture is the Rupture,
  the seal breaks; the stall guard breaks it when no pack but the boss's stands as a fight ends.
  A sealed boss cannot be ordered onto and joins no fight. Pressure keeps running after the
  Rupture. Salvage is the tally times a rate (`Campaign.SALVAGE_PER_MENACE`, set so a battle
  banks about what it banked); Anathema is the tally by domain, recorded beside the count of
  three bosses, which still decides the final.
- **The campaign** (`sim/campaign.gd`): a rift keeps the tier it opened at (`rift.tier`); the
  battle takes it and the live Convergence as its ramp; the finale at the top tier. The quick
  campaign reads the same tables (the Fractures by tier, the elites' slots from the elite
  compositions, the formations as the tier's formation compositions in turn, the density by its
  battle number as the Convergence), a convenience and never a read.
- **The bots** (`sim/battle_bot.gd`): the turtle persona holds the rows before the gate and
  fights only what reaches it; the detaching persona sends one machine at the nearest patrol
  while the body fights the nearest formation. `tools/campaign_run.gd` reports Fractures
  reached, turns to the Rupture, the stall guard's share, spawns by mouth, elites fought and the
  gate's damage by turn.
- **The screen**: the header's Fracture count, SEALED on the boss's readout and its ring inert,
  the tears drawn as small vortices with the one clock's count, the seam read from the field
  (`Bleed.gradient`), the members on a pack's tap, log lines at a Fracture, the Rupture, a tear
  and an elite's arrival. A pass of its own, judged running on the phone.

## The build order

1. This brief, committed. 2. Compositions, menace and the elites in content and the content
database, with the validator and the content tests. 3. A pack as a composition through the
engine and the save. 4. The ramp table, the field at the start, the cycle, the timer, the seam,
the tears and the wall. 5. The Rupture meter, the Fractures, the elites' spawns, the seal and the
stall guard; salvage by menace; Anathema. 6. The campaign's tier lock and ramp, the quick
campaign on the shared tables, the two personas, the runner's reads; both reports rerun. 7. The
records (the bible's rows, the rules, the vocabulary, the README, the status, the to-do, the
measurements), the proposals and this brief retired, the bible republished.

Each step lands with the suite green and its own commit.

## How it is measured

- The campaign runner at 40 runs, plain, turtle and detaching: Fractures reached a battle,
  turns to the Rupture, the stall guard's share, spawns by mouth, the gate's damage by turn,
  salvage banked, the turn the turtle breaks; in `docs/measurements.md` as a direction. The
  turtle read decides whether the interval step moves and whether the bounty cap leaves the
  bench.
- The matrix's composition cells by domain and tier at 200 seeds, on the desk machine (the
  to-do), read as bands: a patrol costs one machine about a tenth, a formation a four about a
  tenth and a trio a fifth to a third, the boss's formation the exam.

## The bench

Ideas a rule kills here, one line each with the rule, so Ryan can overrule the rule.

- A loss when the seam reaches the gate: a round cap by another name, and a loss with nothing to
  fight is the helplessness section 2 removes.
- A forced boss (Loop Hero): the boss's fight takes every machine and nobody leaves, with no
  battle withdrawal to retreat into.
- A rule on flattened hexes (no recharge, spawns anywhere in it): Ryan, 29 October, art only.
- The battle's salvage capped at the rift's bounty, shown on the card: against "a kill is a
  kill"; the cleanest kill of the farm if the menace weighting leaves it worth doing.
- A long battle bleeding the other fronts: against "a turn is one battle".
- The seam swallowing the map's loot as it advances (FTL's fleet): the one form that adds a
  decision; waits on loot lying on the map.
- A ramp that resets at a tier step: with the tier locked and the ramp live, waiting for the
  step would make an old rift easier.
- A wider map now: the grid row (nine is the thumb's limit, the map must not get smaller);
  parked as a finale lever with horizontal scroll.
- Elites as one of the random spawns, the Fracture's pick as loot, a tick per fight or per
  round, a round cap, packs keeping their damage, a formation as two packs: the foes proposal's
  bench, which stands.
