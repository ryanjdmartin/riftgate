# Brief: the Armadillo as a form-changing tank (rework)

The session settles the Armadillo's rework into a form-changing tank at tier one, from Ryan's
framework of 15 October (below, "The Minerva session"), aiming for the simplest rule text.

**Status**: rework, in session (15 October): Ryan's framework set, its details open. **Maker**:
Minerva. **Proposed tier**: one. **Read first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`;
`docs/roster_analysis.md` section 2 (the Armadillo's rating);
`content/chassis.json` (armadillo) and `content/components.json` (lockdown_shutters,
shoring_jack); the bible's rows on the Faraday Hull and the Armadillo's retaliation (section
7), and on Guard, Braced lasting the round, retaliation and thorns (section 5).

## Where it stands in content

An assault walker: hull 150, armour 15, five hardpoints, the locked Lockdown Shutters (a
self-only shield whose rule, Sealed Live, extends the Faraday Hull to blows on the shield), and
the Faraday Hull as its passive (a foe that strikes its bare hull is Shocked). The runner reads
it as nearly indestructible alone, which is a diagnostic only. The bible's open row: Ryan, 12
October, would remove the Faraday Hull first if a retaliation must go ("armoured, not a
porcupine").

## How the go got here (14 and 15 October)

- Ryan: "I wonder about pushing the Armadillo into tier 2 just so that tier 1 doesn't have
  access to a hypertank. That was part of my problem with the Faraday hull, it works as a
  support passive but what if the Armadillo was just the HYPERTANK in the game? And we move
  faraday to a new unit that still wants to get hit, but actually has to worry slightly about
  its own hull (Echidna/Hedgehog/etc), whereas the runner shows the armadillo is pretty much
  indestructible."
- Claude: a hypertank nobody shoots protects nobody, since the exposed aim weighs the hull a
  blow would reach; proposed a conditional draw on the locked shutters (blows at an ally's bare
  hull turn on it while they stand).
- Ryan: "If the armadillo is the hypertank, how do we make it's build interesting other than
  'slap all the lures on it?'" Then: "the shutters closed, protecting the carries, is going to
  be the default. Because it doesn't have a combat passive, there's no reason for it to try and
  beat them at their own role. What if it was a form shaper? Like shutters closed, defenses go
  up, and heat rises every turn? Still needs a payoff, and a build hook."
- Claude proposed the Faraday Hull as the sealed form's payoff. Ryan: "You're sure that
  enabling the shock hull during defensive mode isn't double-dipping? Luring on a tank is kind
  of the whole point, the optimal play, if you will, and rewarding it for doing it's job seems
  potentially dangerous." Claude agreed and withdrew it: under a draw every foe chooses it, so
  a Shock per striker scales with the enemy's blows, which is the old discharge's shape.
- Ryan: "As a form changing tank, the dillo could have a place in tier one as a simple
  role-filler... how can we push it to increase build diversity? The simplest way to implement
  the form change is via spending a weapon or shield, which would free up a locked component
  slot... The other option is to turn on the Faraday hull when it's NOT in defensive mode, but
  I still think that a thorns tank is only balanceable if the thorns themselves have a
  drawback."
- Claude: the form follows the die (a shield spent seals it, a weapon spent opens it); the
  build hooks are every maker's shield as a way to seal, Braced persisting while sealed (the
  longer Braced the bible allows once it carries a drawback, here the heat clock), vents and
  cooling for the open rounds, and lures while open. On the Hull in the open form: with armour
  15 the exposed aim rarely picks it, so it would be too little there and too much under any
  draw; its home is the hedgehog (see `minerva_hedgehog.md`).
- Ryan: "what if, on transforming back from defensive mode, it got a bonus not based on heat
  gained, but punishment endured? Like a kinetic capacitor? Not necessarily to unload extra
  damage, as that could put us back where we started, but as another buff that could be the
  build-around? And are we sure that it should blanket taunt in defensive mode, giving it's
  allies a free pass for what could be 3 or 4 turns? We could have the heat increase faster
  based on hits taken, but a form changer still needs to be able to shift for more than a turn
  at a time or it can get tedious to play."
- Claude proposed every ally Braced per blow endured on opening, and a draw limited to blows
  that would injure an ally. Ryan: "Yeah this is taking off into complicated territory I think.
  And giving the team tank stats is just as toxic as taking all the blows - it's an unkillable
  team by a different name. We need to think outside the box."

## Ryan's shape before the session (15 October)

In his words: "The dillo hits a shield and transforms, building heat every turn. While
transformed, it gains braced (base armour needs to go down), and stores the unmitigated damage
it would have taken as a count on a buff token. Perhaps all its shield faces somehow become
lure faces? Then, when it unseals, it caches in the stored damage for...? A team buff? A self
buff? Something that's a build around that isn't just having it's cake (tanking effectively)
and eating it too (dealing damage)."

Claude's reading of it, for the session to confirm: a shield spent seals it, a weapon spent
opens it, anything else keeps the form; sealed, its shield entries are spent on a foe as lures
(the foe and its neighbours turn on it), the shield's number landing on itself, so the draw is
a spend that shows on the intent each round, never a blanket.

## Claude's proposals for the cash-in (15 October)

- **Lean: rerolls for the team, this round**, a reroll per so much stored, on top of the three.
  The one currency in the game that is neither hull nor damage; nothing gives it to the team
  today (the Clydesdale makes one for itself); it makes the squad's dice better without making
  the squad harder to kill or harder-hitting, and every reroll is still a decision. It composes
  with big dice and rolled weapons (the Muzzle Velocity Booster and the Tactical Databank sit
  on it), answers the Waste's theft of rerolls, and saturates rather than multiplies, since a
  fifth reroll is worth little and none can be banked. It makes "open first" a decision: unseal
  early in the turn and the team rerolls this turn's dice with the bonus.
- Alternatives if rerolls read too abstract for Minerva: Disrupt on every foe that struck it
  while sealed (soft control, the thinnest class, but it overlaps the jellyfish); or a round of
  Indirect reach on its guns when it opens (the turret rising over the shell; worth nothing
  against a pack with no back rank).
- Ryan refused rerolls in the session (below); the payout became optional.

## The Minerva session (15 October)

**Claude's first round**, in chat: every shield it spends is aimed at one foe, which turns on
it, the shield landing on the Armadillo and sealing it; a weapon that deals damage opens it
(lures, shocks and probes do not, so the sealed form keeps a control kit); sealed, it is Braced
(two stacks) and its heat climbs 20 a round; base armour 15 down to 5; the Lockdown Shutters
kept locked, Sealed Live gone, the shutters carrying the charge (blows on the sealed shell
charge it by their number; opening gives the team a reroll for every 20, at most three). Holes
raised with it: sealed without a draw protects nobody (the exposed aim skips a braced shell), so
the draw is essential; the store rewards being struck by the enemy's blows unless the payout is
capped; the heat clock forces venting rather than opening, since a vent spent while sealed
would keep the seal.

**Ryan's framework**, in his words: "Armadillo - lets work with this framework, aiming for
simplicity of rules text:
-Locked piece, a lure with rapid, no cooldown. One or a pair, not sure. the 4 weapons ports are
the 4 hardpoints.
-Shields on self enter defensive mode, gaining braced. Shields still work while sealed. While
sealed, heat rises.
-Damaging weapons and vents unseal it, losing the braced buff. This lets a weaponless build be
possible, if a player so chooses.
-Ally targeted shields, or ally/self targeted buffs, have no interaction with sealed/unsealed.
-Optional: Mark foes that hit it while sealed. When unsealing, debuff marked foes somehow,
ideally with something that stacks per hit. Can be a new unique debuff. Can be tied to the
locked piece(s) only if rules text is becoming a premium. Only worth considering if the
tanking/luring is feeling thin.
-Don't do rerolls as the reward, that doesn't fit with the theme."

What it settles: the draw is a locked lure, Rapid with no cooldown, in place of shields aimed at
foes; the four weapon ports are the four hardpoints; a shield on itself seals it and keeps
working while sealed; a vent opens it as a damaging weapon does, so the clock does force
opening; nothing aimed at an ally, and no buff, touches the form; a build with no weapons is
allowed; the Lockdown Shutters as a component and Sealed Live retire (the shutters live on as
the look of the seal: the pinned "Lockdown Shutters made real").

**Claude's second round**, in chat: one lure with the Klaxon's reach on a five-entry die, the
pair the lever (a pair never chains two lures in one roll, since the free reroll skips
identical entries; "no cooldown" matters only when the team's rerolls fish for it); the seal's
Braced a fixed two stacks, base armour 5; heat +20 at the end of each sealed turn, Burning's
timing, so from cold the fourth sealed turn overheats; allies' vents can hold it shut, and a
Rapid vent of its own can open, cool and reseal in one turn; the free lure works open too, so it
is best spent sealed; the mark held back, its candidate a weaken laid on opening (attackers
breaking their teeth on the shell) sharing Penance's word.

**Ryan, second round**, in his words: "armadillo - no, the braced should not stack. the granted
braced is tied to the sealing, the "defensive mode." Was your point about the lure to make it a
spread lure, but NOT rapid? The mark - my lean would be to make that foe a take damage per stack
the next time it attacks the armadillo, encouraging the player to focus luring the same foe.
The other idea I had was to consume the mark for cooling if the armadillo attacks a marked foe,
which would help it cool down to re-seal again. I like that, actually. If we did that, then
there's a very good play pattern of seal, generate marks, unseal, consume marks to cool,
repeat. The trick is how do we generate marks, just on hit, on the locked lure, or both?"

What it settles: the seal's Braced is fixed, tied to the defensive mode, and never stacks; the
mark is in, as cooling: an attack on a marked foe spends its marks to cool the Armadillo, so
the loop is seal, mark, open, cool, seal.

**Ryan on rerolls** (15 October, in the session): "Rapid should reroll to any other face, not
any other non-identical face, that's incorrect if that's how it works. Stacking a bunch of the
same rapid component is a valid, if silly, build." And of the team's rerolls: "That's not how
the team rerolls should work either, that's so incredibly abusable." Built the same day (the
bible's rerolls row): every reroll skips only the entry showing, a copy included. So a pair of
lures does chain (lure A, then B, one in five on a six-entry die), which corrects Claude's
second round. Raised in chat and open: with no cooldown, two copies can bounce back and forth
(A onto B, B onto A), and a die of nothing but no-cooldown Rapid pieces never ends its turn;
Claude's proposed limit is that Rapid's free reroll never lands on an entry already spent
this round, which changes nothing for today's pieces (each switches itself off).

**Claude's third round**, in chat: Braced agreed, a fixed amount tied to the mode, more
shields adding shield and never Braced. The lure keeps Spread and Rapid (and a pair only made
a lure show more often, which the reroll call above corrected: a pair chains); if a free
three-foe draw is too generous, narrow it to one foe before touching Rapid. The mark goes with
cooling: the blows the seal took pay for the heat it built, and never more than that, since
cooling below zero is wasted; damage on the marked foe's next blow at it is thorns on a braced
tank, the discharge the 12 October row retired. Marks from blows on the sealed shell, a mark a
blow, not from the lure: a free Rapid lure on three foes would make the cooling unearned, and
both would count twice. Only a weapon that deals damage spends marks, or the lure (a weapon of
no damage) would cool the shell without opening it. The passive at the edge of the budget: "A
shield it spends on itself seals it: Braced, and 20 hotter a round. Each blow on the sealed
shell marks the striker. A vent opens it; so does a damaging weapon, whose hits spend their
targets' marks, cooling it 10 each." Smaller holes: marks die with their foe, so an ally's
kill wastes them; a Spread gun on a marked row cashes the row, so the die should show the net
heat; a build with no weapons vents to open.

**Ryan, third round**, in his words: "And I agree about the infinite cantrips. Rapid with no
cooldown needs a limiter. Here's what I'd propose: more heat, since heat is now the
armadillo's resources. Armadillo locked component: rapid, no cooldown, lure 1, 5 heat. Not
sure if it needs 2 or not, depends on the odds. The stack it applies should recover a
significant chunk of heat when consumed, not sure how much. And still not sure if that stack
should be applied as part of the admittedly overloaded passive, or attached to this lure
module."

What it proposes: heat is the limit on a Rapid piece with no cooldown, paid every spend; the
locked lure is Rapid with no cooldown, one foe, 5 heat; a mark spent cools a significant
chunk.

**Claude's fourth round**, in chat: heat holds as the limit (past the maximum each lure costs
5 hull at the turn's end, and it holds only while nothing in the chain cools), so Claude's
spent-entry rule is withdrawn; a pair, since the sealed shell protects nobody without a lure
(one lure on a five-entry die shows 20% of rounds, 40% after a reroll; a pair 33% and 60%),
and Rapid makes the second copy cost no tempo (each hardpoint still comes up a quarter of the
rounds); the mark on the lure, since one foe and a price answer the third round's two
objections, the passive keeping the form alone and the mark's own codex the cooling; a mark
cooling 15. Scorched runs the lure hotter, a weapon of no damage being a weapon.

**Ryan, fourth round**, in his words: "Yes, the component is lure at any reach: an indirect,
damageless weapon with lure and rapid as keywords, costing 5 heat. Things we already support.
Let's try two of them, with the effects you said. Are you sure 15 is enough, with the passive
heat gain as well as the 5 from luring? Take max heat and passive cooling into account as well
(obviously the passive cooling is disabled while sealed, easier math that way and that's how a
player would expect it to work without needing to be told). Those numbers are not fixed,
either, so play around with some math and see if there's a better setup." Then: "Regarding the
debuff, should it be exclusive for the dillo to pop, or should any ally be able to benefit?
And just so we're clear, the mechanic is: hit foe with a damaging weapon, consume all stacks
to cool X heat per stack?" Then: "I think the only rule that we have to enforce is: Rapid
always has a limiter, via cooldown, heat, or volatile, which encompasses our existing rapid
pieces as well. Even then, a rapid vent of any kind seems like a free way out of the heat
system, so I would be very wary about it." And: "I'll take suggestions on what the lure and
the debuff should be called. Probably not the klaxon, just because that's sound-based, and
sound doesn't seem like something that would give you heat back. make sure to check our
component naming conventions before suggesting anything."

What it settles: the locked piece is a pair, each an Indirect weapon of no damage with Lure
and Rapid (no cooldown) at 5 heat, marking the foe it lures; no passive cooling while sealed;
the mark's mechanic, a damaging weapon's hit on a marked foe spending every stack to cool so
much a stack; and a rule for every piece (the bible's Rapid row): Rapid always carries a
limiter, a cooldown, heat or Volatile, with a Rapid vent of any kind a free way out of the
heat system that Ryan is very wary of.

## A heat model (15 October)

A toy model of the Armadillo alone, not the game's runner (the seal is not built). The die:
the two lures and four hardpoints (two shields, two guns). The round as the rules run it, with
no cooling while sealed, the seal's heat at the end of each sealed turn and the excess over
the maximum burnt as hull. The player spends every lure; holds the seal until its next turn
would pass the maximum, then opens with a gun that cashes the marks (or a vent); spends one of
the team's rerolls a round fishing for a lure; and loses the marks when an ally kills the
marked foe, one round in seven. 4,000 fights a row, of 8 rounds (a meaningful fight) and of 16
(a boss). The share of rounds it ends sealed, with the marks and (in brackets) without, and
the average sealed stint in rounds:

| Seal a round | Heat max | A mark | Sealed, 8 rounds | Stint | Sealed, 16 rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 60 | 15 | 56% (36%) | 1.9 | 51% (22%) |
| 20 | 60 | 30 | 61% (36%) | 2.1 | 58% (22%) |
| 15 | 60 | 15 | 66% (46%) | 2.5 | 60% (29%) |
| 15 | 60 | 20 | 68% (46%) | 2.7 | 63% (29%) |
| 15 | 80 | 20 | 74% (58%) | 3.3 | 69% (35%) |
| 10 | 60 | 15 | 76% (63%) | 3.5 | 71% (41%) |

Read: heat starts every fight at zero, so the heat maximum is the first stint and the marks
are what let it seal again. A cash of two or three marks empties most of the gauge whether a
mark is worth 15 or 30 (the rest is lost below zero), so the mark's number barely moves an
8-round fight and matters in a long one. The seal's heat is the lever: 20 a round with no
cooling gives stints under two rounds; 10 lets it sit sealed most of a fight with no marks at
all. Overheat stays under 10 hull a fight in every row; a build of one shield and a vent seals
about 13 points less and almost never overheats; a gun running 10 hot costs 4 to 6 points;
without the reroll it lures less and overheats more. The script was a throwaway; this
paragraph is its whole description.

Ryan, 16 October: "Currently, we allow allies to cool the armadillo while it's sealed. Does
that affect your math at all?" The same model with allies cooling the sealed shell during the
player's turn, at a seal of 15 a round, heat max 60 and a mark of 20. A teammate shows its one
vent a fifth of the rounds; the Puffin's whole die on the shell cools it about 8 a round (its
passive sheds 10 a shield, the Wake Diffuser vents 10, two of its five entries give nothing,
and each Rapid entry switches off):

| Allies cooling the sealed shell | Sealed, 8 rounds | Stint | Sealed, 16 rounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| None (the model above) | 68% | 2.7 | 63% |
| One teammate, its vent given half the times it shows | 71% | 2.9 | 67% |
| Three such teammates | 76% | 3.5 | 73% |
| The Puffin's whole die | 81% | 4.2 | 78% |
| The Puffin's whole die and two teammates' every vent | 91% | 6.3 | 90% |

Read: an ordinary team stretches the seal a little and the marks still do most of the
cooling; a team that spends everything on the shell nearly removes the clock, paid for by the
Puffin doing nothing else and the hitters giving up their vents. The Puffin is the partner to
watch, since its cooling rides on things it does anyway (a shield on the shell, a Rapid vent).
At a seal of 20 the all-in team reads 82% with stints of 4.4, but the Armadillo alone drops to
59% with stints of 2.0, so the seal stays tuned on the Armadillo alone.

## Refused along the way

Not to be proposed again without a new reason:

- The Faraday Hull on it in any form (a Shock per striker under a draw is the discharge again;
  in the open form the aim rarely picks it).
- A blanket draw while sealed (with armour 15 a swarm's blows bounce entirely and the Meadow is
  deleted; Ryan's "free pass for 3 or 4 turns").
- Team Braced or team shield on opening (an unkillable team by another name).
- Unloading the charge as damage (Ryan: "back where we started").
- Heat per hit so fast that a seal lasts a turn (tedious to play).
- Rerolls as the payout (Ryan, 15 October: "that doesn't fit with the theme").

## Holes already raised

- Sealed without a draw protects nobody: the exposed aim weighs the hull a blow would reach, so
  a braced shell is skipped unless something draws the blows (the locked lure, in Ryan's
  framework).
- The heat clock is the seal's limit and the number the stock pass tunes.
- The greedy bot values a lure for allies at nothing, so the runner's rows wait on its
  valuations.

## Pairs and dependencies

- The echidna (`minerva_hedgehog.md`) was to take the Faraday Hull; Ryan's Sonic Needle took its
  place there (15 October), so the Hull and Sealed Live leave with the Armadillo's old kit.
- The shield rule (decided): shields are honest numbers now, so the plate it seals with is the
  shield's number.
