# Brief: the hedgehog, the Faraday Hull's new home (new chassis)

The session designs a lighter Minerva tier two that wants to be struck and pays for it in its
own hull, carrying the Faraday Hull off the Armadillo. "The hedgehog" is a working label from
Ryan's own list; the name is his.

**Status**: direction. **Maker**: Minerva. **Proposed tier**: two. **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `minerva_armadillo.md`; the bible's rows on the Faraday Hull (section
7) and on retaliation and thorns (section 5); `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 8 (Minerva's
gaps); `content/components.json` (lockdown_shutters, proximity_klaxon,
retribution_thurible).

## What Ryan said

- 14 October: "we move faraday to a new unit that still wants to get hit, but actually has to
  worry slightly about its own hull (Echidna/Hedgehog/etc), whereas the runner shows the
  armadillo is pretty much indestructible."
- On thorns, 15 October: a thorns tank is balanceable only if the thorns carry a drawback of
  their own; rewarding a tank for doing its job (being struck under a draw) is dangerous (said
  of the hypertank).

## What Claude proposed

- 14 October: a lighter Minerva tier two struck by its own choice: a locked lure to bring the
  blows in, and the Faraday Hull (a foe that strikes its bare hull is Shocked) for the team to
  cash. Low armour makes the exposed aim choose it, so the Hull fires often, and its hull is
  what it pays: the drawback the thorns need. A lure keeps the multiplication a spend rather
  than a standing state. Two Minerva tanks that read apart: the Armadillo the wall, this one
  the bait that punishes.
- 15 October, why the Hull lives here and not on the Armadillo: Shock on a striker lands no
  damage at the blow and must be cashed by a team spend, so it is capped by the team's dice
  rather than the enemy's blows; but under any draw on a heavy machine it is too much, and on
  armour 15 without a draw too little. On a light machine with a lure it fires often and costs
  hull each time.
- The analysis (section 8) had first pencilled Minerva's tier two as "the line", every weapon
  luring; the hedgehog took the lure line when the Hull moved here.

## Related: the Retribution Thurible

The analysis (section 2) found the Thurible (a buff: for two rounds a foe that strikes the
machine's bare hull is Shocked) gives any machine the Faraday Hull's effect, stocked on the
Nemesis. With the Hull on the hedgehog, the Thurible duplicates the hedgehog's passive.
Claude's lean: it should differ on purpose or become a rare tier-three piece, and the Nemesis
stock something of its own. The session decides this with the chassis.

## Holes already raised

- Under a lure, the Shocks a round equal the lured foes that wound it, so the lure's reach is
  the lever that keeps it off the discharge's shape.
- The Onryō also reads a foe's blow on hull, and the Nemesis banks rage on it; three machines
  reading the same wound should part by payoff (the Hull a doubled team blow, the Nemesis
  tempo, the Onryō its curse).
- The greedy bot values neither being struck nor a Shock laid for an ally, so the runner's rows
  wait on its valuations.
