## The kit, as it now stands

- Medium, armour 0, shield 10, hull 24, move 4, four hardpoints and the locked piece. His words:
  "Base Armour: 0. Shield above average, hull average (as you see fit)". The numbers are
  placeholders.
- **Sonic Needles**, the passive: "Striking a foe, or getting struck by one, grants that foe a
  stack of Sonic Needle. Sonic Needle - Conducts Chain. On 3 stacks, the Echidna fires Chain 2,
  then consume all stacks on this foe."
- **The Supersonic Spike Tether**, the locked piece: "Indirect, Sap 1, Lure, 1 Heat."
- **Spiker**: first the Hypersonic Spike Tether, Instant at 2 heat; then the needle saps 1 a
  stack. **Chaos**: first a bolt reduces the stacks to 1 (his change of 21 November: "reduce
  stacks to 1"); then its attacks deal +1 per stack of Sonic Needle.

## The look so far

His pick of 15 November is the armoured car: "I like the armoured car". It has six heavy wheels,
each in its own boxed housing, and a faceted hull with a wedge prow and a low turret. It turns on
the spot like a tank, skid steer, so the housings stay rigid. The spines need not be "so on the
nose" ("remember the machines were nicknamed after animals they resemble or act like").

The second sheet (`docs/looks/minerva_echidna2.png`, built by
`tools/lowforge/looks/echidna_looks.py`) shows the bristle three ways:

- **A, the bristle as its kit**: two banks of six thin dark rods with lit tips on rails along the
  engine cover, and one emitter off the prow. The rods were a guess at the Sonic Needle's
  emitters. With the kit set, the needles are what it fires and leaves in its foes, and the prow
  emitter could be the Tether's barrel.
- **B, the bristle as its armour**: a comb of seven raked plates across the engine cover,
  growing taller and narrower toward the tail.
- **C, barely there**: the plain car, with two grilles, a spine rib and three whip aerials.

Claude picked B on 15 November, because its side silhouette is the bristle and it reads from
behind. With the kit set, A now has a reading it lacked before: the needles and the Tether
mounted on the car. His caution on A was that the rods can read as a rocket rack from the side.
