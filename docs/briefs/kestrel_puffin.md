# The Puffin: a rework

<!-- hangar: {"kind": "chassis", "maker": "kestrel", "tier": 1, "status": "rework", "slot": false, "name": "Puffin", "built": "puffin"} -->

Kestrel's tier-one shield escort, built and fielded (the bible's Puffin row, Ryan's go of 21
October: its shields read the ally's heat; the Slipstream Projector grows a shield spent on an
ally by one for every two heat the ally carries, the Backwash Ionizer an overshield of three on an
ally), sent back to the drawing board on 9 November by the refit pass (`docs/briefs/refit_pass.md`):
shield faces leave the die, so a shield-bot has no die to be.

## Ryan's direction, 9 November

"Puffin - send it back to the drawing board for now, but note that this is probably an
opportunity to make it more interesting than the shield-bot it was before. Something heat and/or
cooling related, since Puffins are cold-water creatures, my first instinct being the only unit
able to cool its allies."

## Where it stands

- Under the refit pass's model cooling is passive and no vent face stays on the die, so a piece
  that cools an ally is a verb nobody else has: a locked piece on the Puffin, the one way heat
  comes off a machine faster than its own cooling. His instinct, for his session.
- The Puffin keeps its body, its model and its place in Kestrel's ten; its kit is the question.
  Everything proposed before (the shield that reads heat, the overshield) retires with the shield
  faces; the heat reading is the thread worth keeping, turned round: it read an ally's heat to
  shield it, it would now take an ally's heat off.

## Holes raised, for the chat first

- The model's principle is no pure recovery face on the die. An ally's cooling is a recovery
  face by another machine's hand. It stands as an identity only because it is unique and ally-
  only, a support verb like a Barrier on an ally rather than housekeeping; it must stay the one
  such piece, or the principle is gone.
- Its worth rides on heat biting. If the prices and the cooling leave a stock die cold (the
  heat grid of 28 October found heat free on one), a cooler of allies is a dead face; the
  Puffin is read after the heat prices land, not before.
- What it takes off and where it goes: a flat cool, a cool that pays something (the heat turned
  into a blow, a shield, a reroll), or a cool that moves the heat onto the Puffin, which then
  carries it as its own brake. The third is the most Kestrel: risk carried for the team.
- Whether it keeps a defensive verb beside the cooling (a Barrier on an ally), or is the pure
  cooler and leaves the Barrier to the basics.

## What the session hands back

Its kit as a first round in chat, Ryan's marks, then content with the refit pass's content, its
runner row beside the Storm Petrel's and the Skua's, its model unchanged.
