# Brief: the Dingo (rework)

The session settles the Dingo after its harpoon's change: its tier, and its die as a team
piece.

**Status**: rework; the harpoon's Shock in content (Ryan, 14 October; built 15 October), the
tier open. **Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler. **Proposed tier**: one or two. **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 2 (the Dingo's rating); the bible's
rows on the cars (section 7); `content/chassis.json` (dingo) and `content/components.json`
(megataser_harpoon).

## What is decided

The Megataser Harpoon carries Shock in place of Suppress (Ryan, 14 October: "Dingo - lets
change it, and im not sure about the tier decision"). The pack animal tases the foe its ally is
about to eat, and the ally's blow lands double: the first locked piece that amplifies for
others, and the Baboon's meter still counts it. It takes the only hard-control piece off a
tier-one chassis without a rule. In content since 15 October, ahead of the stock pass.

## Its tier

- The reshuffle (Claude, 14 October) proposed tier two: a pure team piece, which the sort puts
  at tier two, and the harpoon a stun below tier two.
- After the Shock (Claude, 14 October): the budget's reason for tier two is gone; the one
  reason left is that the tender would deal a pure team piece. Claude's lean: leave it at tier
  one until the stock pass, when Wainwright & Sutler's tier-one pool is known.
- Ryan is unsure.

## Where it stands otherwise

Two actions a turn on the map (the Retrograde Flywheel); light, wheeled; alone it loses to
every pack but two (a diagnostic; a support frame is expected to lose alone). The analysis
rated it a strong map niche on a thin die, its die the stock pass's.
