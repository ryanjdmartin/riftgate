# Brief: the Dingo (rework)

The session settles the Dingo after its harpoon's change: its tier, and its die as a team
piece.

**Status**: settled in chat (Ryan, 19 October: the winch as a swap of two foes or spaces, tier
two with it), the go pending; its tier moves in content when the revolver or the fan-boat can
be dealt, so the maker's tender deal never fixes on the Prairie Dog. **Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler. **Proposed tier**: one or two. **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 2 (the Dingo's rating); the bible's
rows on the cars (section 7); `content/chassis.json` (dingo) and `content/components.json`
(megataser_harpoon).

## What is decided

The Megataser Harpoon carries Shock in place of Suppress (Ryan, 14 October: "Dingo - lets
change it, and im not sure about the tier decision"). The pack animal tases the foe its ally is
about to eat, and the ally's blow lands double: the first locked piece that amplifies for
others, and the Baboon's meter still counts it. It takes the only hard-control piece off a
tier-one chassis without a rule. In content since 15 October, ahead of the stock pass.

## Its tier

- The reshuffle (Claude, 14 October) proposed tier two: a pure team piece, which the sort puts
  at tier two, and the harpoon a stun below tier two.
- After the Shock (Claude, 14 October): the budget's reason for tier two is gone; the one
  reason left is that the tender would deal a pure team piece. Claude's lean: leave it at tier
  one until the stock pass, when Wainwright & Sutler's tier-one pool is known.
- Ryan is unsure.

## Where it stands otherwise

Two actions a turn on the map (the Retrograde Flywheel); light, wheeled; alone it loses to
every pack but two (a diagnostic; a support frame is expected to lose alone). The analysis
rated it a strong map niche on a thin die, its die the stock pass's.

## Since then

Ryan, 18 October: "Dingo is pretty solid but we might be able to jam something else in there to
add some interest, maybe two locked components, not sure." Claude's candidates, the same day,
one line each: the winch, pairing with the harpoon as the Badger's claw pairs with its
fragmenter (pin the foe, then pull it into the front rank where every Direct gun reaches it; its
hole: a foe moving between ranks is engine work Minos also waits on, and it does nothing against
a lone boss); a locked Disrupt flare (the foe changes its plan, then the harpoon doubles the
ally's blow on it; cheap in the engine, two control verbs on one car); a locked ally-only vent,
the water cans, the Coolant Dump's old job (thematic, small, no decision beyond whom); or one
locked piece and the stock pass carrying the interest. A second locked piece reads as tier two by
the sort (a pure team piece), which settles the tier with it (the maker's tiers in the README).

Ryan, 19 October: "Dingo winch is perfect. We could increase it's flexibility by allowing
lateral movement as well, unique from the Minos. Target two foes/spaces and swap them? Useless
against a boss, sure, not sure if that matters." Claude's reading, the same day: the general
swap is the piece and the whole rule ("target two foes or spaces and swap them"), distinct from
Minos by pattern (Minos turns whole ranks over involuntarily; the Dingo chooses two). It exposes
a caster to Direct fire, sets the acting order (which foe is the next to act beside another,
for the spider's web and a stun's timing) and bunches foes for every area piece (the fan-boat's
shells, the strafer's spray, the Boar's spill), so a swap then a shell is a cross-maker combo:
the aspiration of feeding other makers' pieces met by one locked piece, and soft control.
Useless against a lone boss, which does not matter for a tier-two team piece whose harpoon
still works there. Edges for its row: a massive foe fills its column and does not swap, so
bosses are out by rule; a swap with an empty front slot is a pull. Engine: a foe moving
between slots, which Minos's rank turn also needs. Ryan, 19 October: "Dingo seems good like
that." Later that day: "Dingo - probably isn't a real winch since that doesn't make sense.
Can't go that low tech. More teleporter -ey": a cable cannot swap two things, so the piece is a
teleporter in the maker's register, which for a teleporter is wire, the telegraph. Claude's
candidates were Switchboard and Telegraph Transposer; Ryan, 19 October: "a little too
nonsensical. Something more like 'guys this totally isn't just a teleporter.' Like Combat
Translocator or something." The piece is the Combat Translocator (his working name).
