# Brief: the jellyfish, the Science Vessel homage (proposal reworked into a direction)

The session refines Aphelion's jellyfish into the roster's soft-control specialist and settles
the dispel it carries.

**Status**: direction (14 October); the proposal was unfinished. **Maker**: Aphelion.
**Proposed tier**: two. **Read first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`;
`docs/aphelion_chassis_proposal.md` section 4.2 and Ryan's calls in section 3;
`docs/roster_analysis.md` section 12; the bible's rows on the dispel and on Numbed (section 5).

## Ryan's calls before the go (12 October)

The Stingray goes deep on status, this one goes wide; "Could specify 'all non-damage effects'
to narrow the range and bring the fantasy out of obvious damage carry applications"; a dispel
is needed and the EMP is core to the homage ("I'm not concerned about the keyword count";
getting back up can be changed if needed); it flies, so a jellyfish name.

## What the go said

- Ryan, 14 October: "science vessel changed to our soft-control specialist?"
- Claude, 14 October: agreed. Its spread would carry Lure, Disrupt and the dispel to the foes
  beside the target, and nothing else. Its EMP then reads as dispel plus Disrupt rather than
  Shock, which is what an EMP does to a plan, and it is never blank, since every foe has a move
  to lose. The two Aphelion tier twos split cleanly: the Baboon counts statuses, the jellyfish
  changes what the enemy does. It also keeps hard control out of the spread: with Suppress in
  it the spread would triple a stun beside the Paradox in the same house.
- Ryan, 14 October: "neat, good direction, needs refinement".

## Open for the session (the refinement)

1. The EMP's verbs: dispel and Disrupt both, or one.
2. A spread Lure turns three foes onto a light flyer: its risk, and a decision, or too much.
3. Disrupt on a foe with one move is only a target redraw, so its worth rises with the foes'
   fresh start.
4. The dispel's name (Ryan's word was "dispel"; Strip and Purge were offered), its exact reach
   (the foes' own buffs; Numbed out of reach), and its carriers.
5. Its stock and its designation.

## Waits on

The foes' fresh start (foes with more than one move make Disrupt worth spreading).

## What the session hands back

A proposal in chat; on Ryan's go, the dispel into the vocabulary, the bible's rows, content
planned, and this brief retired.
