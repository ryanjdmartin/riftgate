# Brief: the thunder figure, the Arsenal's smiter (new chassis)

**Ryan's direction (6 November)**: Soft Protoss Archon homage. Unit with lots of shield and low
hull. Weapon hits grant it a stack of Empowered, but maybe cost it shield - either a flat cost or a
%current, not sure. Shocking a foe also gives it Empowered, maybe. It ramps up throughout the fight,
as long as it keeps attacking to keep its stacks up through the decay. Locked component stops decay
for a round, but not sure what to staple that onto - a shield is too self-sufficient, a weapon just
turns it into the best roll, so maybe a self buff or debuff? Maybe two - one self buff, one that
shocks all foes and grants a stack of Empowered for each shock it applied, from the passive.

Other look - it gains empowered on weapon hit or shock, but empowered stacks burn its shield either
at end of turn, or as they decay, something like that. That's more thematic.

The session designs the Arsenal's half of Ryan's shock-synergy unit of 4 November, its thunder
demon or god. Aphelion's half is Static (`aphelion_static.md`). Its figure is Ryan's to name.

## Claude's first round (4 November)

**What stands.** Shock and Chain are Aphelion's lean ("most Shock and Chain setters land in its
content"). Today's setters are the Polarity Projector, the Tesla Gauntlets (Minerva's), the
Megataser Harpoon (the Dingo's), Retaliate, and Charged, which has no carrier yet. Crossover is
welcome (Ryan, 28 October: "Leans, not rules, not rails").

**A smiter, not retribution.** Claude's first lean for it was Retaliate on every ally. That is the
Onryō's trigger (a curse on whoever wounds the team) and close to Misharu's (a foe that breaks a
shield is punished), so the Arsenal would hold three machines punishing the striker; it is
withdrawn. So the thunder figure is Chain's capstone at tier three. The rule it bends: its arcs
carry its blow's whole number. Its locked piece is an area Shock setter: the bench's bell tower
toll, a big Indirect blow with Spread, Penetrate and Shock, set aside on 11 October for the four
briefed machines with no other reason recorded. That toll is also the Spread Shock setter the
to-do says Chain still lacks. The thunder figure feeds Aphelion's Static and is fed by it.

**Forms already set aside.** The kill and repeat (Ryan, 28 October: "a no-go"). The charge to a
random foe (retired 3 November).

**Its figure.** The Arsenal names its machines for figures who punish, never war; a thunder that
strikes the guilty fits the family. Raijin, Thor and Zeus are BattleTech 'Mechs, on the reserved
list.
