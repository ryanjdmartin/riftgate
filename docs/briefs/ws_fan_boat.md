# Brief: the fan-boat (proposal, back to the board)

The session reworks the fan-boat hovercraft, whose weapons all fire its deck gun's shot with
their own keywords, so that its identity shows at its tier.

**Status**: proposal, back to the board with its tier (the analysis, 14 October). **Maker**:
Wainwright & Sutler. **Proposed tier**: one in its proposal; two in the analysis and the
reshuffle. **Read first**: `docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/ws_chassis_proposal.md` (the
fan-boat's section, the magazine among its alternatives); `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 4.

## The design (the proposal)

"Everything it fires goes through its deck gun: each weapon fires the deck gun's shot, with its
own keywords." Locked: the deck gun, Direct and flat. Ryan, 13 October: the shells, "and see
where it takes us".

## Why it went back (the analysis, 14 October)

A tier-one chassis stocks basics beside its locked piece, and basics carry no keyword by
definition. At the tender the fan-boat would be four entries all firing the same flat shot, its
passive invisible until the first keyword blueprint is found; a tier one must show its identity
with basics alone. Two ways back: tier two, where it stocks tier-one keyword pieces and its
passive shows from the first fight; or a locked piece that carries the first keyword. The
proposal's own question points the same way: whether the magazine (the deck gun firing with the
last three keywords its other weapons fired) makes a better tier two.

## Open for the session

2. The shells or the magazine.
3. The proposal's holes: Rapid shells at the deck gun's number are its strongest build (the
   deck gun's number is the lever); a shell's own heat no longer counts.
