**Ryan's direction (11 November)**, in the fast flyers' round (`kestrel_line.md`, The fast flyers):

> Skua: Ramping Dakka. Complete rewrite:
> Passive: Damaging an enemy grants it a stack of Ricochet (once per turn).

> Ricochet: When striking an enemy with a weapon, the weapon strikes it again for each stack of
> Ricochet, dealing 1 damage each time. Lose half stacks of Ricochet on any hull damage.

Kestrel's tier-one strafer, built and dealt (the bible's Kestrel row, his go of 21 October; its
first brief, `git show f87b831:docs/briefs/kestrel_strafer.md`). Today: a light flyer at move 6,
hull 20, shield 12 at a regen of 2, a d4 of the locked Flechette Pod and three hardpoints. Its
Split-cycle Breech doubles the hits of every weapon and equipment it aims at a foe and halves each
hit's base, rounded down and never below one; the Pod sweeps, a shot a column. He had kept it for
a session of his own (11 November: "Clydesdale and skua I'm handling in other sessions"); the
rewrite came in this one. It stays dealt as built until a go.

## What the rewrite replaces

- The Split-cycle Breech goes whole, and its engine with it: the split strikes, the halving and
  its rounding, a debuff hitting twice.
- The Flechette Pod is no lock: an ordinary weapon in its stock, so Sweep is in the pool for
  any machine (his question of 10 November, "it just depends if we want Sweep to be a generally
  accessible targeting type or not", answered).
- Its lock is the Hypersonic Swoop, equipment spent on itself.

## What follows from the rules as he wrote it (Claude's reading, built so unless he says)

- **Ricochet's extra strikes are strikes**, his word: separate blows of the weapon at a number of
  1. So each pays the foe's armour, and a point of plate stops every one of them: the old Skua's
  counter, whole now. Each takes the firer's Empowered and the foe's Flak, a point a stack each.
  Each carries the weapon's riders, a numbered one landing a hit (Burn, Flak, Cryo). An Exposed
  foe's armour counts for nothing, so the mark is its way through plate.
- **A stack from the Swoop is a buff like any**: a foe's Purge takes stacks of it (a stack is a
  stack).
- **An overheat is hull damage**, so its own heat halves its stacks as a foe's blow does.
- **Stacks last the fight**, as a status does: every fight opens on none.
- **Rapid carries a limiter**: the Swoop prints a cooldown of one, dark for the rest of its round,
  the pool's standard, unless he wants its price in heat.
- "Once per turn" is once a round; the stack lands after the spend that earned it, so that
  spend's own strikes do not ricochet off it.

## Holes raised, where his words give two answers or none

1. **A strike of 1, or exactly 1.** "Strikes it again" is a strike through the whole blow (the
   reading above); "dealing 1 damage each time" could mean a plain point that nothing adds to and
   no armour stops. It decides both its counter and its ceiling: as strikes, plate stops it and
   riders multiply by the stacks; as plain points neither.
2. **For each enemy a spend strikes, or for each strike.** A weapon of several strikes multiplies
   under the second (four strikes on five stacks is twenty more). Claude's lean: once a spend on
   each enemy a strike was aimed at, so a Sweep's shot at each column counts for its own foe and
   an area's other foes, reached and not aimed at, do not (as Shock reads a strike).
3. **The half it loses**: rounded which way (is a lone stack lost?), and at each blow that takes
   hull or once a round. Three bites through the shield are 8 to 4 to 2 to 1 under the first.
4. **No cap.** One or two a round for as long as the fight runs: two by the third round, which
   is a squad's whole fight at tier one, and eight or more against a boss. If the loss is the
   cap, nothing more; the lever is a ceiling.

## Watch, no question

- The ramp is felt late. Its fights as a flanker are short, so the Swoop, one entry in four, is
  what makes it felt early; its long fights are where it is the hitter.
- It ramps only with a clean hull, on a shield of 12: a pack that out-hits its shield in a round
  keeps it at nothing. Kestrel's risk, and the Shoring Jack in its stock earns its place once
  the shield is down.
- The roster's ramps: the Onryō and the thunder figure are his directions of 6 November, and
  "enough growers" (20 October) was said of growth on every roll.
- Its place on the fast flyers' map moves from the wide fight to the long one; it is wide only
  while it carries the Pod.
