# Brief: the Kestrel line (review, 20 October)

The review of every Kestrel chassis, built and proposed, under the action economy's direction
(the bible's row beside the stun row, 20 October): where each stands, what would improve it,
and the holes in Kestrel's stock that the stock pass should fill. Ryan asked for it on 20
October, after the direction went to main: "revisit all the Kestrel chassis and look for
areas of improvement, and flag any stock holes that need filling." Everything here is
Claude's proposal for him to move; each chassis's improvement lives in its own brief and is
only pointed at from here (the interceptor got a brief of its own, `kestrel_interceptor.md`).
Ryan, 20 October, on reading it: the kits written out whole, not as bullet points, and the
stock by kind, since most stock pieces are placeholders until the stock pass; so every brief
now carries "The kit, written out" (the Goshawk's and the Puffin's below), its stock by kind
and a piece named only where it fits as it is.

**Status**: proposal (Claude, 20 October). **Maker**: Kestrel. **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md` (the taste lines, the tiers table, the action-economy section); the
bible's action-economy row, its leans row and its Kestrel rows (sections 5 and 7); the five
Kestrel briefs; `content/chassis.json` and `content/components.json` (maker `kestrel`);
`docs/balance.md` (13 October: the Kestrel rows and the pick shares).

4-4-2). Each with what it reads, what it produces, what it pays, and the currency the
direction gives it (the row's homes: strikes, a die mechanic, a banked resource, a cooldown
burst, a delayed action, a combined entry, own rerolls).

**Kestrel's identity, re-read under the direction.** The leans row (13 October) gives
Kestrel "Rapid, Overcharged and Overshield, and the chain rule (tempo, burst, escort)". The
line as it stands reads otherwise: Kestrel's currencies are heat as a resource (the Goshawk
banks it, the Afterburner and the Sparrowhawk spend it, the off-tank burns shield in its
place), the die as a gamble (the platform's growth, the Sparrowhawk's d3, the strafer's
spray), rerolls (Ryan, 19 October: Kestrel's), strikes (the strafer) and speed on the map
(move 50, field recharge 30: the detachment). Rapid is its gated cantrip, not its spine.
Ryan, 20 October: "the maker didn't really have an identity before other than the look, and
now we are indeed looking at heat, rerolls, multi-strikes, and risk". The leans row carries it
since that day; Overcharged leaves the Kestrel lean (the Coil is its one carrier, and the
Ammit's talons and the Reliquary are the Arsenal's). Three of the eight hover (the Puffin, the
off-tank, the capital) and five fly; the capital hovers from irony (Ryan, 20 October).

**The overlap map**, each pair parted by its shape (Ryan's rule of 18 October): the strafer
and the Sparrowhawk, count against magnitude; the strafer and the platform, strikes doubled
by a passive against strikes grown by rolls; the off-tank and the Goshawk, shield burnt for
damage and plate against heat banked for shield; the interceptor and the Sparrowhawk, a
second spend every round against a chain on a kill; the interceptor and the Puffin, its own
second spend against a vent that gives an ally its turn; the bomber and the platform, the
slots against the rolls; the Goshawk and the Sparrowhawk, hot sustain against hot burst.

**The tender.** Three tier ones (a hitter of volume, a body, a tempo hitter), so Kestrel's
deal varies by seed and the off-tank holds a front, Ryan's reason of 19 October (a tier-one
body from more than one maker).

**The data** (13 October, the old line): the Kestrel team loses the Meadow formation (39
percent greedy, 83 percent of its hull), the Marsh (7) and bleeds on the Peak (72, at 72
percent of its hull): no body. The Goshawk alone wins only the fights a hitter wins (1 percent
against the goblins, 14 against the gels, 41 against the needlers), the weakest of the
hitters, which the review reads as its stock's verdict (below), not its passive's.

## Each chassis: where it stands, what would improve it

One pointer each; the proposal is in the brief named.

- **The strafer** stands as proposed for the go. The hole re-flagged: with basics alone it
  gains nothing against bare foes and loses against plate; the root is the scales question
  (section 12), never an exception; its payoffs are riders, flat buffs and multi-strike
  pieces, cross-maker by design, and the stock holes below give Kestrel one of its own.
  `kestrel_strafer.md`, the line review.
- **The off-tank** stands as Ryan's package, a hovercraft at 50 (Ryan, 20 October: fast, so
  Kestrel is seen to make a fast one; a decent generalist that can zoom off and fight a pack).
  The plate's arithmetic read against the foes: a wall against the packs that strike for 10
  (six of the eighteen wildlife), no wall against a swarm (six goblins strike 30 each with
  their swarm, 25 past the plate); the lever named. Its lock is the direction's model combined
  entry; never Rapid. `kestrel_shield_spender.md`, the sixth and seventh rounds.
- **The interceptor** had no kit, and its first (the first weapon Rapid, the dive a gun of
  15) Ryan read as thin with no build hook (20 October). The second kit: a weapon of no damage
  is Rapid once a round, so every control piece of every maker is a cantrip on it; its lock the
  designator dive, a weapon of no damage spent on a back-rank foe through a standing front at
  the front foe's blow, which lights that foe for the team's Direct guns this round; a
  tilt-jet (Ryan, 20 October). `kestrel_interceptor.md`.
- **The Goshawk** stands; its stock is cold for a heat machine (its hottest stocked gun pays
  10, the Afterburner's base nothing), so its loop barely runs with its own kit: about 15
  heat carried, 5 shield a round. Proposed for the stock pass: the Overclocked Railgun to its
  stock (off the Sparrowhawk's, as the pass already plans), a mid-heat Kestrel gun (below),
  the Pinion Driver out (a blueprint, the Sparrowhawk's rework); its niche word in content
  ("Ramping damage") rewritten to the sustain it is (flagged 18 October). Its thinness as a
  partner (no state given, none needed) is accepted: the Kestrel that stays.
- **The Puffin**: Ryan worries it is toxic and boring (20 October) and wants it more Kestrel;
  its brief (`kestrel_puffin.md`) proposes the Ionizer reading heat, an ally's overshield as
  big as the heat it carries. The Wake Diffuser landed on Ryan's go the same day: Rapid off, a
  vent of 10 that shields 10 (the shield half with the combined entry's engine), which on the
  Puffin's passive vents twice and costs the turn; and Rapid vents or shields, if any exist,
  never below tier three (his rule of the day). The engine fires its passive once a spend,
  whatever a shield's strikes (noted for the capital's lock).
- **The Sparrowhawk** stands as its brief left it; the direction reads its chain as a
  cooldown burst gated by a kill, the row's own shape. The caster-killer niche (its open item
  2) goes to the interceptor's dive if Ryan takes it; the Sparrowhawk is the trash chain and
  the steady doubled gun. Its cold hard hitters are other makers' by design (no Kestrel hole).
  The records it moves on its go: the to-do's stock pass line (the Pinion Driver fixed on the
  Sparrowhawk, the Goshawk's hardpoint a basic) and the bible's stock-pass row.
  `kestrel_sparrowhawk.md`, the line review.
- **The bomber** stands. Two items: the mark's "Rapid, cooldown 1" is the switch-off, the
  ungated form the row re-prices (and the brief's "a free mark every other round" misreads
  the cooldown), so a gate is proposed: Rapid while a mine waits on the field, a setup gate
  of the Paradox's shape; and Chain reopened in mines, so the Arc Fault Cannon is Kestrel's
  own bomb (the 15 October reading made Lure, Disrupt and Chain inert because the round they
  work in is over, which is true of the two intents and not of a kill's repeat).
  `kestrel_bomber.md`, the line review.
- **The gun platform** is Kestrel's capital ship, a hover, not a flyer (Ryan, 20 October,
  from irony: three quarters of the line flies), and Ryan asked what niche it fills other than
  more guns. Its sixth round answers: the fleet's reroll economy (the team's unspent rerolls
  banked, up to three; growth on every roll of its die; a second lock that relaunches a jet's
  die), four hardpoints and two locks, assault, the ekranoplan. Settled here: no multi-strike
  vent in the pool; the own reroll a Kestrel module's (below). `kestrel_gun_platform.md`.

## The Goshawk's and the Puffin's kits, as the stock pass would leave them

The two built Kestrels have no brief; their kits are here, the passives and fixed pieces as
built and the stock by kind (Ryan, 20 October: the stock pieces are placeholders until the
stock pass, so a piece is named only where it fits as it is).

**The Goshawk.** Tier two, a medium flyer at move 40. Hull 100, shield 50, field recharge 25,
heat maximum 100, cooling 5, armour 0; five hardpoints beside the fixed Afterburner Cannon, a
d6; two module slots. The passive, the Thermal Recoupler, as built: at the start of its turn
it regains 5 shield for every 10 heat it carries. The fixed piece, the Afterburner Cannon, as
built: Direct, flat 10, heat 0, fires the whole heat gauge into the shot at +10 per 10 heat
fired, and the airframe is cold after.

| Entry | Piece | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| I | the Afterburner Cannon | the dump: 70 at 60 heat, then no regen next round |
| II | a big hot flat gun (the Railgun, if the pass keeps it; its note calls it temporary) | the regen's fuel |
| III | a mid-heat gun (a stock hole) | the regen's fuel |
| IV | a warm flat gun, or Kestrel's chain gun | the cool round |
| V | a shield | |
| VI | a vent | the second brake, with cooling at 5 |

Today its stock carries about 15 heat a round (a warm gun, the chain gun, a gun of no heat, a
shield, a vent), 5 shield a round, which is why its solo rows are the weakest of the hitters;
the Pinion Driver leaves it as a blueprint. The round as played: fire hot; at the next round's
start cooling takes 5 and the regen reads what is left (40 → 20 shield, 60 → 30); ride hot for
the regen or dump the Afterburner and be cold next round; the vent when the gauge nears 100
and the Afterburner is not showing. A hot stock (a 40 and a 20 a round) sits at 60 by round
two, a tank's regen at tier two under the honest shield rule, with the overheat at 100 the
ceiling. It wants hot guns of every maker, the Peak's Scorched, the Thrust Overboost (its guns
twice as hot: twice the regen for twice the risk) and the Boundary-layer Bleed; it gives
nothing to others, the Kestrel that stays, and the Puffin's vent fights it. Its niche word in
content ("Ramping damage") is stale; its 5 per 10 is re-read under the honest shield rule by
the stock pass.

**The Puffin.** Tier two, a light hover at move 40. Hull 50, shield 30, field recharge 30, heat
maximum 60, cooling 5, armour 0; four hardpoints beside the fixed Backwash Ionizer, a d5; two
module slots. The passive, the Slipstream Projector, as built: any ally this machine shields
also sheds 10 heat. The fixed piece, the Backwash Ionizer, as built: shield 30, allies only,
Overshield (past the ally's maximum; the excess fades at the round's end).

| Entry | Piece | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| I | the Backwash Ionizer | 30 on an ally past its maximum; it sheds 10 |
| II | the Overcharged buff (the Coil today) | the hitter's round; the strafer counts it twice |
| III | a shield, self or ally (the Deflector at its new number, or a plain one) | the hurt ally |
| IV | a vent that shields (the Diffuser as a combined entry: vent 10, shield 10, no Rapid) | the hot ally sheds 20 and gains 10, and it costs the turn |
| V | a small gun | when nobody needs anything |

The round as played: the Ionizer on the machine the intents name; the Coil on the hitter
before it fires; the vent-that-shields on the hot one; a gun otherwise. It wants shields of
every maker (each a vent through its passive) and hot allies; it gives the off-tank its
magazine, the Sparrowhawk's 30 hull an overshield and its chain's heat vented, the
interceptor's two spends cooled, the strafer's doubled Coil, and the Goshawk a vent it does
not want (one pair of seven). Alone it loses, by design. The engine fires its passive once a
spend, whatever a shield's strikes.


Kestrel's pool today: ten components (one fixed, the Afterburner; one locked, the Backwash
Ionizer; the Pinion Driver leaving for the pool as a blueprint) and three modules. Every
Kestrel weapon is Direct, flat and single-target, Chain aside; no multi-strike piece, no
rider, no Indirect piece, no rolled gun, one hot gun (the Railgun at 40 heat, which its own
note calls temporary) and nothing between it and the Carbine's 5. What the eight builds want
that the pool does not give:

1. **A Kestrel multi-strike gun**, tier one or two: flat and hot, 5 ×4 at heat 15, a rotary
   cannon on a jet (the Goshawk's inspiration carries one). The strafer's (×8 under its
   passive), the platform's payoff (growth on every strike), Flak's partner. Parted from the
   Maxigun (rolled, cool, three strikes). The to-do's stock pass already names multi-strike as
   the blow currency; this is Kestrel's one.
2. **A mid-heat Kestrel gun**, tier one: flat 25, heat 20, Direct: the step between the
   Carbine and the Railgun. The off-tank's middle burn (20 shield for 20 more and a zap of 20),
   the Goshawk's regen fuel, the Sparrowhawk's doubled 50 at 30 heat.
3. **A Kestrel reroll module**, tier two: one free reroll of this die a round, its stat line
   paid (heat maximum −20, or shield −20), per the row (own rerolls as a number, priced so no
   module is the universal pick). The platform's second roll, the Sparrowhawk's fishing for
   its live lock, the Armadillo's for its seal or lure.
4. **The Wake Diffuser**, landed 20 October on Ryan's go: a vent of 10 that shields 10, no
   Rapid (the shield half with the combined entry's engine, the to-do). **The Laminar
   Deflector** follows his rule of the same day (a Rapid vent or shield never below tier
   three): Rapid off at its tier, with a Kestrel twist in its place, the lean a shield that
   reads heat (20, and 5 more per 10 heat this machine carries: bigger on the hot, the
   Goshawk's and the Sparrowhawk's, parted from the basic by the clause); or the piece to tier
   three with its Rapid, found only. Section 12's question.
5. **A big hot flat gun kept in the pool** for the off-tank's and the Goshawk's builds (the
   Overclocked Railgun, if the pass keeps it: temporary by its note since 26 September), in the
   Goshawk's stock rather than the Sparrowhawk's.
6. **Chain in mines**, a rule change, so the Arc Fault Cannon is a bomb (the bomber's brief).
7. **Seven locked pieces to name**: the spray, the lure that regens itself, the dive, the reset
   gun, the three-strike shield, the pull and the mark.
8. **Not holes**: no Kestrel Indirect piece (the dive is Kestrel's reach, priced, where the
   artillery makers' is free) and no Kestrel rolled gun (the Muzzle Velocity Booster serves
   other makers' rolled guns on Kestrel frames, the cross-maker pull); no Kestrel plain shield
   (the basic shield is shared; Overshield is the Puffin's); no Kestrel cold hard hitter (the
   Sparrowhawk's pull of Minerva's and Wainwright & Sutler's kinetics is its design).

## The two free slots

Two toward Kestrel's ten, later maker sessions'; the bench for them, one line each:

- The capacitor (18 October, kit 1): overshield as its ammunition, a bank of safety that pays
  damage; tier two beside the Puffin, which feeds it. Ryan passed over it for kit 2 without
  striking it.

  field recharge, the shield recharging from damage dealt): a map identity; benched until the
  map can make it a decision (the four-machines row sends the roster to one front).
- Struck: the chain tier three (Rapid switches nothing off on it), the Rapid pile the row
  forbids; its exit: a pool where every Rapid piece is gated bounds it, so reopen if the
  stock pass gates them all and the shape still reads.
