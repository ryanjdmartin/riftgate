## The fast flyers, under the long wall (11 November)

Ryan, 11 November:

- "Given our new battle framework, I want to settle the identities of the Kestrel fast
  flyers. Not pigeonholes, but just what does what and why you'd want one over the other."
- "Kite, Skua, and Needletail are the 3 move Sixers, where the skua has a direction but needs
  tweaks and the needletail is basically free to be whatever we want. Buzzard is close behind
  as a move 5 risky dps/burst engine, giving it a hybrid identity that can fit into either a
  squad or flanker role."
- Of the Kite and the Buzzard: "I feel like the "cousin" angle between kite and Buzzard can
  be dropped since they no longer share names."
- Of the last two slots: "the remaining two Kestrel chassis probably shouldn't be in the move
  6 light space with these three. Probably more in the Buzzard/storm Petrel medium space."

**What the wall asks of a fast machine** (the rules' battlefield). A high-move machine is the
flanker and the firefighter (his word of 5 November). A formation is a fight for three, a
lone patrol a duel for one, a pair or a hunting pack a fight for two, an elite and the boss
a true four. A machine has one action a turn, its hull stays for the battle and its shield
is full as every fight opens. So what a flank sends comes in three shapes: many small heads,
one head that matters, and a fight too long for a light.

**The map.** His word leads each row; the rest is Claude's reading, his to move.

| Machine | His word | Its fight | It wins by | What it cannot do | What its kit wants |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Kite, tier two, move 6 | "Burst glass cannon" | the short one, and the first rounds of any | every weapon doubled on an unhurt foe, the Pinion Driver its finisher, a kill recharging its shield | anything big after its first shot; a long fight | one big single hit; reach |
| The Skua, tier one, move 6 | "Ramping Dakka" | the long one | Ricochet: a stack a round for damaging a foe with a weapon, each stack a strike of 1 more | plate; a pack that out-hits its shield | riders a hit, flat buffs, Exposed |
| The Needletail, tier two, move 6 | "free to be whatever we want" | beside another machine | two things a round, a weapon and an equipment, its mark every round | weight of its own | every maker's equipment |
| The Buzzard, tier two, move 5, a medium | "risky dps/burst engine" | either, squad or flank | every foe that falls feeds its shield, and its guns zap the shield into their blows, its cannon firing it whole | a lone boss, which feeds it nothing | falls: a squad that kills fast, spread damage |
| A medium, unspecced | "at least one chassis that orbits" Exposed | its session's | cashing the mark the others set | | |

In time they spread: the Kite is strongest in a fight's first round, the Skua in its last,
the Buzzard when it chooses, and the Needletail every round alike. They pair on Exposed, the
maker's keyword: a painted foe's armour counts for nothing, which is the one thing a strike
of 1 fears, and the paint puts an untouched back rank in reach of the Kite's Direct opener.

The Kite, the Skua and the Needletail were rebuilt on 11 November on his go, on placeholder
numbers. Their briefs are retired (the briefs' README lists each with its place in git), the
bible's Kite and Kestrel rows carry what he decided and what he declined, and his play of
each is a line of the to-do's.

## The last two slots

The Magpie and the Snowy Owl, marked by Ryan on 21 October and each briefed. Both are mediums
by his word of 11 November (above), and their tiers are open: both at tier two under 3-5-2,
or the support engine at tier one under 4-4-2.

What either session must hold:

- A third Kestrel support parts by its shape from the Puffin, the Giant Petrel, the Storm
  Petrel and the Needletail.
- One of the two is the chassis that orbits Exposed, by Claude's placing of his word: three
  locks set the mark and no Kestrel machine reads it.
- Stealth is new to the rules. Phased is shelter, out of reach of both sides, so a hidden
  machine is a state of its own.
- The Syrinx Drone, the mimic's voice box, stays on the Lammergeier.

The bench for either, one line each:

- The machine that must fight to stay shielded (Ryan's version, 18 October: the shield
  coming back from damage dealt). Benched then as a map identity. Under the reboot it would
  be a shield that comes back without the turn, which his expectation keeps to a kit or a
  locked piece.
- Growth on every roll, the gun platform's first shape: Ryan, enough growers.
- Struck: a chain tier three, on which Rapid switches nothing off, the Rapid pile the action
  economy forbids. It reopens if every Rapid piece in the pool is gated and the shape still
  reads.
- Taken: the capacitor, a bank of shield that pays damage. The Buzzard took that shape on
  18 November, its shield spent into its blows.

## The names round (21 October, evening)

A stub for the picks: every name is content's, and four have moved since (the Skua's passive
is the Carom Field, the Lammergeier's the Reverberant Payload, the Storm Petrel's the
Hardlight Generator and its lock the Hardlight Pulse). What a later name must hold:

- Kestrel's register is flight and aerodynamics, with a bird inside where one fits (the
  Pinion Driver, the Syrinx Drone). A stock piece may be a rifle; a locked Kestrel piece
  never is (Ryan, 11 October).
- A designation follows the pattern content shows: K, an odd number, a mark and what the
  airframe is (K-13 Mk I Swing-Wing Strafer). It is shown text, so his to pass.
- Every chassis name is checked against `content/reserved_names.json`, which is how the
  Butcherbird turned out a BattleTech 'Mech; a plain Owl is a Heavy Gear machine, so the owl
  keeps its species.
- Words taken elsewhere: Translocator (the Dingo's), Talon (the Arsenal's), Phase
  (Aphelion's word, and Phased), Rift (the enemy's), Pathfinder (a BattleTech 'Mech),
  Tesseract (Warhammer 40,000) and Slipspace (Halo).
