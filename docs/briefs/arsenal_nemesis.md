# The Nemesis: a rework

<!-- hangar: {"kind": "chassis", "maker": "arsenal", "tier": 2, "status": "direction", "slot": false, "name": "Nemesis", "built": "nemesis"} -->

**Ryan's direction (11 November)**: "Nemesis might need reworking to a smooth resource bar like the
swallowtail. Taking a hit on shield gives 1 rage, hull damage gives 2 rage, and its rage is consumed
in chunks of 5. Complete placeholder numbers. It could be niched as a tank buster: getting its
attacks completely blocked gives 2 rage, and consuming rage gives the punch breach, with the rapid
being an upgrade option."

The Arsenal's tier-two brawler, built and dealt (the bible's Nemesis rows). Today its Crucible
Capacitor banks a rage for each hull hit, up to five, and its two locked Knuckles of Fury spend a
rage to roll the die again (a plain roll, no switch-off: "it's not a choice, you just get rapid",
Ryan, 12 October). Under the refit pass's model its shield regenerates, so hull damage, its one
fuel, comes rarer and the rage starves. It stays dealt meanwhile.

## The direction, as it reads

A bar that fills by smaller steps (a hit on its shield, more for a hit on its hull, more again for
an attack of its own blocked whole), spent five at a time to make the Knuckle's punch breach: a
tank buster. Rapid, today's whole product, becomes one direction of its upgrades (the locked
pieces proposal: two directions, two steps deep, on the Knuckle).

## Holes raised in chat the same day (Claude's)

- **It retires a Decided row**: "Rage buys actions, never damage: a content call for this machine."
  The form that failed is the roll again fed by hull damage alone, starved by the regenerating
  shield. The row is rewritten when the rework lands, naming that.
- **A blow that breaks its shield and reaches its hull** is two (Ryan, 11 November: "hull damage
  takes priority, so it would be 2 if the shield is broken and hull damaged"). A blow its armour
  or a Barrier takes whole is neither a shield hit nor hull damage, so it banks nothing.
- **Spent automatically** (Ryan: "Rage is spent automatically"): a full chunk always breaches the
  next punch, whatever it meets, as the Muster's charged pieces spend it.
- **Breach passes armour, never a Barrier**: a foe's Barrier eats a breaching punch as any weapon's
  (rules, the Barrier), the chunk spent and the block paying two back. Ryan, 11 November: "Barrier
  isn't a tank property, so I'm not sure if it should break it. Probably not, but note it down."
  His lean, noted: the punch leaves a Barrier whole.
- **Blocked whole**: armour soaking all of a blow and a Barrier popping both count, by his words.
  Still open: whether "its attacks completely blocked" reads a spend (two for a punch that lands
  nothing on anything) or each blow of it (two a blow, so a spread or a multi-strike piece banks
  more). Claude's lean: a spend.
- **The bar's cap**: five today; spent in fives, a cap of ten holds two (Claude's, a placeholder).

## What the session hands back

The bar's rules and numbers, the Knuckle's two directions, the row rewritten; its runner row
against the armoured and the barrier-raising formations.
