# Brief: the revolver (in session since 18 October)

The session finishes the chicken walker whose die steps to the next entry instead of rolling,
and settles its locked piece and its tier.

**Status**: in session (18 and 19 October), five rounds in; a damage dealer by Ryan's call, his
kit settled (the sixth shot, the Spin, the pips, the buff), hull and the buff's name open, then
the go.
**Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler. **Proposed tier**: one (the reshuffle and this session); two

8), its Rapid and rerolls rows (sections 7 and 6), its heat row (section 5) and its one-off

## Where it stands (18 October)

Every number is a placeholder; names are Ryan's, the working ones marked.

- **Passive** (Ryan's wording, 12 October): "Whenever its die rolls, it moves to the next entry
  instead, in sequence." A reroll is a roll, so it steps; a lock holds it; Rapid's free reroll
  steps it; a foe's Scramble is a true roll, and the sequence runs on from where it lands.
- **The spin** (Ryan, 18 October): each fight opens on a random chamber, then the cylinder
  steps. The plan is a cycle: what the player sets in the hangar is adjacency (the shield
  before the big gun, the Rapid before the chamber it should double), not which round a
  chamber lands.
- **The dial**: a team reroll steps it a chamber (the others locked), so three rerolls reach
  any of four chambers in a round at a shared price, and the spin is random the player can
  steer.
- **Chamber I** is the locked piece by the die's fixed-first rule; the player orders the five
  hardpoints around it (VI before it, II after).
- **Rapid**: a Rapid chamber fires, switches off for the round and steps on; a run of Rapid
  chambers fires in order, each once, the whole die the ceiling (the limiter rule, 15
  October). No rule on the revolver: the chain's price is heat, the stock pass's (below).
- **Role** (Ryan, 18 October: "revolver and boiler are getting promoted to damage class"): the
  maker's gunslinger, a damage dealer: its locked piece is its gun, the chain its tempo, and it
  gets a body's hull to hold the tender's front (Claude's lean 100 or 110).
- **The sixth shot** (Ryan's kit, 18 October): "every sixth shot counts double: its damage and
  the stacks it lays" (Burn becoming stacks applied directly, Ryan, 19 October, so the clause
  earns its text; Vitriol likewise; nothing to say of Shock or Suppress). The count is weapon
  spends only, reset each fight; spins and equipment are not shots, so the player steers which
  chamber takes the sixth. Shown as six pips under the heat bar (Ryan, 19 October: the best
  spot), the sixth lit when it is next.
- **The Spin** (locked, Ryan's working name): no damage, Rapid with no cooldown and no penalty,
  its reroll a true spin of the cylinder over all six chambers, itself included (Ryan, 19
  October: the high roll, allowed as an exception to the reroll rule, a design one-off and no
  balance one); it grants a unique stacking buff on the machine, +5
  damage a stack, spent whole by the next shot and carried between turns if a turn ends without
  one (Ryan, 19 October; the name his). Its limiter is the cylinder, not its own: it cannot
  chain itself, and the way back to it in a round runs through the other chambers' switch-offs
  or the team's rerolls (Ryan: a combo piece, not a typical Rapid one, and the stepped barrel
  prevents its abuse); the Rapid row says so at the go, with the exit that a stepping-die piece
  that ever makes a free chain gets a limiter of its own.
  The two-strike Hogleg is retired (Claude's name and shape from the retired proposal; no
  reason for the double hit).
- **Numbers**, the proposal's placeholders: medium walker, move 40; hull 90 (Claude's lean:
  100 or 110 if it is the tender's front), shield 40, armour 5, heat 60, cooling 5, field
  recharge 20. Five hardpoints, a six-chamber die. Stand-in stock at tier one, basics only:
  Twin Autoguns, Howitzer, Blast Door, Ankle Mortar, Coolant Jacket.
- **Showing it**: the die's places are numbered (the Roman numerals, built), so the chambers
  come free; the next-chamber chip, the hold's ring (the entries in order, the current lit, an
  arrow to the next), the hangar's numbered chambers, the drum clicking round and the codex
  printing the order are the engine's at the go. A destroyed component is a known dead
  chamber every cycle; a cracked one keeps firing.

## Claude's proposal (18 October)

- **The brief's three questions.** The spin in place of the first chamber (Ryan's). Tier one:
  the identity shows with basics alone (a known cycle from the first roll), it is the maker's
  front for the tender, and one machine of three without the roll's drama is the easiest rule
  in the game to learn. The big blow by the calendar: a shield sized to it or a Barrier (the
  Blast Door's fantasy) in the right chamber, the dial lining it up with a countdown's landing
  round; a slot-in Suppress on it is gated by timing by construction, a point for the stock
  pass keeping slot-in Suppress.
- **The root cause** (the one-off row): "Rapid once a round on it" looked reasonable because
  the chain is free. The limiter is a per-piece rule written for a random die, where Rapid is
  "act again, at random"; on a stepping die it is "fire the next chamber too", strictly
  stronger, and the switch-off caps nothing a chain wanted. The 0-heat Rapid pieces (the
  Deflector, the Diffuser) make a chain that fires the whole die every round for nothing. The
  fix is the stock pass's: Rapid priced in heat by its niche (tempo), and the Rapid vent gone
  (section 12's question). Then the chain is a hot burst build that needs vents and overheats
  by its third round, the heat row's own test, and the revolver has two builds: the hot chain
  and the cold cycle.
- **Cooldowns**: Claude's "nearly free on it" withdrawn; they are the gate in the chain, which
  is where the chassis will be played, and the cooldown row stands.
- **The locked piece**, two shapes, one idea each:
  - **A, the hammer cocks** (lean): Direct, flat 10, heat 10, and +10 for each round since it
    last fired, up to +50. In the chain it shows every round and the decision is each round's:
    fire it at 10, or leave it unspent (it steps past) and fire 60 when the boss is Shocked or
    the big foe steps up. In the cold cycle it comes round charged on its own. The reward is
    time, which a fast cycle cannot farm.
  - **B, the fanned hammer**: a strike more for each chamber skipped since it last fired, so
    the team's rerolls are its ammunition and the double hit keeps a reason. Its hole: skip
    everything, fire only this, feed it three rerolls a round, a one-entry machine on the
    team's rerolls.
- **Ryan's kit, analysed** (18 October). Double over twice: two blows lose the foe's armour
  twice, the second finds the Shock spent and a second Suppress lands for nothing; a doubled
  blow keeps it one shot. "Doubles its effects" needs little text: Burn is per 10 of the blow,
  so doubled damage doubles it, and the only stackable rider not derived from damage is
  Vitriol, the spider's passive; "every sixth shot deals double" suffices, with "and lays its
  statuses twice" the line to add if a per-hit slot-in rider ever exists. The sixth on a
  Shocked foe is four times, once a cylinder: a high moment. The Spin can only be read as a
  true spin (a step from chamber I never returns to I in a round). Every way to hit it twice
  in a round, with its ceiling: the spin landing on itself (if a spin may: one in six, about a
  fifth of a spin extra on average); a Rapid piece in chamber VI stepping into I after it fires
  (one in five a spin, each loop spending a switch-off, so n Rapid pieces loop at most n times
  and an all-Rapid die runs out of guns to give the +5 to); a team reroll from VI into I, once
  a round for a shared reroll. The stacked +5s top out around +15 to +25 in the freakiest round
  on a die with nothing left to fire: no exploit in damage, the action economy bounded by the
  other chambers' switch-offs, and the no-cooldown buys the chase Ryan named (spin into a Rapid
  piece into the Spin into the big gun). Its limiter by the 15 October rule: heat 5 a spin,
  nothing to the slow cycle and a tax where the loop lives, rather than an exception. Each Spin
  re-randomises the cylinder's phase, which the opening spin had already made the plan's
  nature (adjacency, not sequence); the random factor stays one, the spin. The hammer-cocks
  and fanned-hammer shapes go to the bench, superseded by the kit.
- **The passive**: one idea, the hook on the locked piece, unless Ryan has a hook in mind. The
  candidate that reads the revolver's own structure: a chamber skipped stays loaded and fires
  twice when it next comes round, which rewards the dial in the cold cycle and gives the chain
  nothing.

## Holes

- The chain is free until Rapid carries heat, so the revolver's balance waits on the stock
  pass's Rapid prices and the Rapid vent's fate.
- The sixth shot's count and the Spin's +5 are counts to show (six dots on the machine, the
  +5 on the die's number), or they are hidden counts.
- The Spin's true spin is one special case in the die mode (a Rapid reroll from this piece
  spins, every other reroll steps), a printed property of the locked piece.
- With the spin, a short fight uses a random window of the cycle; the early-and-late planning
  the fixed opener gave is gone, adjacency remains.
- It breaks the one-source-of-randomness test on one machine, which the one-off rule for
  design allows (section 7); its row should say so.
- The bot spends what shows and never plans, so its runner row is a verdict on the bot.
- B's degenerate loop (above).
