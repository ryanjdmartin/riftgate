# Brief: the revolver (proposal)

The session finishes the chicken walker whose die steps to the next entry instead of rolling,
and settles its tier.

**Status**: proposal with a place (the analysis, 14 October). **Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler.
**Proposed tier**: two in its proposal; one in the reshuffle (not decided). **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/roster_analysis.md` section 4; the bible's cooldown row (section
8).

## The design (the proposal, with Ryan's calls)

Whenever its die rolls it moves to the next entry instead, in sequence (Ryan's wording, 12
October). A reroll is a roll, so it moves one on; a lock holds it. The loadout's order is a
fire plan; each fight opens on the first chamber; a chip shows the next entry. Rapid becomes a
sequence: Ryan, 13 October, a player who slots a Rapid chain and neither overheats nor gets
blown up has earned the broken game, and Rapid's pieces stay small, so the chain should be
funny and rather weak (if not, Rapid once a round on it). Locked: the Hogleg, two strikes.

The rest of the proposal (12 and 13 October; Claude's unless marked Ryan's):

- **The shown text** (Ryan's wording, 12 October): "Whenever its die rolls, it moves to the
  next entry instead, in sequence." The rule it bends: every die rolls.
- **The fire plan**: a stun lined up for the boss's telegraphed round; a duplicate to repeat a
  beat (two Howitzers in six chambers fire every third round); a blank as a known rest. It also
  set "a shield before the big blow", which read the whole-block shield rule (open below).
  Rapid's reroll is a roll, so a Rapid entry fires the next chamber at once, and a Rapid piece
  set before the big gun is a known double. Routes: Rapid pieces of any maker (today the Pulse
  Vacillator, the Wake Diffuser and the Laminar Deflector; the proposal also named the Flakshot
  Repeater, which is the Prairie Dog's fixed piece and fits no other machine).
- **The first chamber**: each fight opens on its first entry, so the plan is the same every
  fight and the hangar shows it. The cylinder keeping its place between fights was set aside: a
  hidden state for the player to carry.
- **Showing the order**: on the die, a small chip at its edge shows the next entry; held, the
  die lays its entries out in order as a ring, the current one lit and an arrow to the next;
  the hangar numbers its hardpoints as chambers; the model's drum clicks round a chamber at
  every roll; the codex prints the order. A foe's Scramble jams it: the next roll lands
  anywhere, and the sequence runs on from there.
- **The cost**: no luck either way: a destroyed component is a known dead chamber every turn.
- **The Rapid chain**: five Rapid pieces and a gun fire all six chambers every round, against
  three and a half on a rolled die; the lever, Rapid once a round on it, gives one double and
  never a chain.
- **Engine**: a die whose roll is a step: the next entry in the die's order (its fixed entry,
  then the hardpoints), a reroll a step, a lock a hold, Scramble a true roll; each fight opens
  on the first entry; the die's next-entry chip, and the ring on the hold.
- **Numbers and kit** (the proposal's placeholders, set under the whole-block shield rule):
  medium, walker, move 40; hull 90, shield 40, armour 5, heat 60, cooling 5, field recharge 20.
  Locked: the Hogleg (a working name), Direct, two strikes of 10, heat 10, the hammer fanned.
  Five hardpoints: a six-chamber die. Its stock, written for tier two with today's pieces as
  stand-ins: Twin Autoguns, Howitzer, Ankle Mortar, Incendiary Shells, Laminar Deflector. The
  stock pass rewrites every stock, and a tier-one chassis stocks basics beside its fixed
  pieces.

## Since then

- A team reroll rerolls every unlocked die, so it steps the revolver whether or not the player
  wanted the chamber moved; the player locks the revolver before every team reroll meant for
  another machine. One tap a round, and it must be taught (Claude, 14 October).
- Cooldowns decided (14 October): on a die that steps, a cooldown of so many rounds is exactly
  so many chambers (Claude), so it is the property's native reader.

## Its tier

The reshuffle (Claude, 14 October) proposes tier one: the sequence shows with basics, and the
Rapid chain's ceiling needs tier-two pieces anyway. The hole: it takes the dice off one machine
of three from the start.

## Open for the session

1. The proposal's question: is the Rapid chain funny rather than strong (if not, Rapid once a
   round on it); should every fight open on the first chamber.
2. Tier one or two.
3. What the fire plan sets against the big blow: the proposal's "a shield before the big blow"
   read the whole-block shield rule; under the honest rule a shield answers a blow by its size
   (the bible's shield row: a shield sized to it, or a Barrier).

## What the session hands back

A proposal in chat; on Ryan's go, content planned (a die whose roll is a step is engine work),
and this brief retired.
