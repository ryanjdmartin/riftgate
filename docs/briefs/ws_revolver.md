# Brief: the revolver (proposal)

The session finishes the chicken walker whose die steps to the next entry instead of rolling,
and settles its tier.

**Status**: proposal with a place (the analysis, 14 October). **Maker**: Wainwright & Sutler.
**Proposed tier**: two in its proposal; one in the reshuffle (not decided). **Read first**:
`docs/briefs/README.md`; `docs/ws_chassis_proposal.md` (the revolver's section);
`docs/roster_analysis.md` section 4 and section 12.

## The design (the proposal, with Ryan's calls)

Whenever its die rolls it moves to the next entry instead, in sequence (Ryan's wording, 12
October). A reroll is a roll, so it moves one on; a lock holds it. The loadout's order is a
fire plan; each fight opens on the first chamber; a chip shows the next entry. Rapid becomes a
sequence: Ryan, 13 October, a player who slots a Rapid chain and neither overheats nor gets
blown up has earned the broken game, and Rapid's pieces stay small, so the chain should be
funny and rather weak (if not, Rapid once a round on it). Locked: the Hogleg, two strikes.

## Since then

- A team reroll rerolls every unlocked die, so it steps the revolver whether or not the player
  wanted the chamber moved; the player locks the revolver before every team reroll meant for
  another machine. One tap a round, and it must be taught (Claude, 14 October).
- Cooldowns decided (14 October): on a die that steps, a cooldown of so many rounds is exactly
  so many chambers (Claude), so it is the property's native reader.

## Its tier

The reshuffle (Claude, 14 October) proposes tier one: the sequence shows with basics, and the
Rapid chain's ceiling needs tier-two pieces anyway. The hole: it takes the dice off one machine
of three from the start.
