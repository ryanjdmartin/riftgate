# The Storm Petrel: a rework

<!-- hangar: {"kind": "chassis", "maker": "kestrel", "tier": 1, "status": "direction", "slot": false, "name": "Storm Petrel", "built": "storm_petrel"} -->

**Ryan's direction (11 November)**: "Storm petrel I don't see as that much of a buff, really. In
fact, it's really a nerf because it can no longer fill its shields with regular components. We
might need to tweak it anyway after the heat pass, though. The thing I want to keep mostly on it is
the shield armour, since it's kestrels one and only tank, kind of. A potential rework would revolve
around that, I would think. Actually, gaining shield when it gains heat is a really solid hook.
That would fuel the shield tank role with risk, and then the Puffin gets a natural buddy. The
shield zap can go somewhere else, either another unit or a module."

Kestrel's tier-one shield tank, built and dealt (the bible's Kestrel row; its engine landed 30
October, its brief retired: `git show d84ad16:docs/briefs/kestrel_shield_spender.md`). Its Riot
Field Generator: while its shield stands every blow on it loses 1 (the shield armour), and its
weapons burn shield in place of heat, the target taking a blow of what the shield paid (the zap);
its locked St. Elmo's Regenerator lures and regrows 2 shield a round for three. Under the refit
pass's model its gauge is 16 with a regen of 1, and no die is dealt a shield entry, so the shield
its guns spend comes back slowly: a nerf, as he says (Claude's audit had called it a cheaper engine).
It stays dealt; the rework waits on the heat pass.

## What stands

- The shield armour: every blow on it 1 lighter while its shield stands. His one keep.
- Kestrel's tank by shield, beside Minerva's by armour (the refit brief's lean).

## The hook, and the holes raised in chat the same day (Claude's)

- **The hook is shield for heat gained from any source** (Ryan, 11 November: "I did mean any
  source"): its guns, Burning, the Puffin's dump. The Boundary-layer Bleed (Kestrel, tier one:
  "Each time its heat climbs, it stands up 1 shield") is no bar (Ryan: "I'm not concerned about
  modules. The best fit for the chassis is the best fit. Plus nothing says that a module can't
  borrow parts of a passive anyway").
- **The Buzzard turns heat into shield too**: its Thermal Recoupler adds to its shield regen for
  the heat it carries (as regen since 11 November). They part by when: the Buzzard by heat held,
  each round; the Petrel by heat arriving, at once. Ryan, 11 November: "different enough, but I
  agree they're similar. If anything I'd think the Buzzard should change, though. It's Regen was
  given when Regen was rare." So the Petrel keeps the hook, and the Buzzard is the one to change
  if they read alike (the bible's Buzzard row).
- **The zap travels with its payment**, and its new form is Ryan's (11 November: "I know the zap
  can't move alone. It would be something like spend X shield and add it to the attack"): a piece
  or a module that spends shield and adds what it spent to the blow, on another machine or as a
  module; its weapons make heat again, so the Petrel pays nothing in shield.
- **The St. Elmo's Regenerator**: his words leave it; its regrowth is a regen grant beside the
  chassis's own.

## What the session hands back

The hook and its numbers, the zap's new home, after the heat pass; its runner row beside the
Puffin's rework (the pair) and the Skua's.
