## Ryan's frame (28 October, his words)

- Heat is "a core pillar of the game, and it's undervalued as a gameplay element".
- "Sometimes I think of the offense-defense-heat triangle as something the player has to work
  hard at to play around efficiently, like a eurogame, cutting one of the sides of the pyramid
  out while still being effective should feel like a huge accomplishment, and the enemy threats
  are the wrench that prevents a fully efficient build from just doing its infinite thing
  forever."
- "It might be easier to think of every component having a price, either in heat, hull, or
  shield, vanilla vents excluded. Possible that cooldown is also a sufficient cost, or certain
  payoff/consumer pieces that are a dead face otherwise don't need an additional cost."
- On heatless basics: "I would disagree with basics having no heat, as that teaches the player
  zero heat as the norm and not how to interact with the heat system. Plus it neuters many
  possible tier 1 chassis and makes others irrelevant (Puffin, for instance)."
- On passive cooling: "Passive cooling was cut, right? If not, maybe it should be? That would
  help push the heat system into something the player MUST interact with, either by playstyle
  or build choice."
- On venting: "I agree with giving the player options other than 'gotta waste a turn venting,'
  although sometimes you do just need a big fat vent. Vent payoffs certainly can have a place."

## The overheat's penalties (30 October)

His calls on the forms an overheat's price could take, made of the overheat that burnt its
excess once. The reboot's stands in its place, and no bible row holds these calls, so a
session that prices the overheat again reads them here.

1. **Burnout**, the component whose spend took the gauge past the maximum burnt out for the
   battle: "yes, makes perfect sense on all counts". It rode the injury threshold, which left
   the game on 15 November, and its switch left with it.
2. **Self-Corrupted** on that component, a stack an overheat, a cleanse its answer: "Also
   makes sense". It is still a what-if of the runner (`--overheat-corrupt N`), off in the
   game.
3. **Disabled** next turn: "don't bother with disabled, the soft punishes above are better".
4. **A weaken**: "Weaken is something the foes might want to carry. Note it down for that
   reason, and I'm not against it here, but yes its more of a hard punish than corrupted".
   The foes' bench has it (the briefs' README).
5. **Escalation on repeat**, a second overheat in a row burning double: "Yeah no point in
   this".

For any form that lands: "Jenny needs to do some lifting, and we need warning chips and such".
Jenny's overheat line, the warning at three quarters of the maximum and the forecast's
overheat warning are built. The read of that day (`docs/measurements.md`, 30 October) is a
bot's under the old overheat: what a price does to its tempo, never how a form feels.

## Open for Ryan

- Whether equipment carries a price in heat: his "every component having a price" against
  the cool play that a buff at no heat and a debuff device at one or two make of it (the
  bible's triangle row).
- The vanilla's heat and what a point past the maximum burns: the HEAT and OVERHEAT dials,
  his to play (the numbers brief, section 2).

  prices rose (the bible's triangle row).

  session.
