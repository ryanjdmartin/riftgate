## The tree as it stands

His shape of 16 November, built for a tier one (two directions, two steps each) and kept in
content as planned drafts, so nothing offers them:

- **Ablate**: "Change damage to Ablate 2 (but it decays), and then second upgrade adds Seared."
- **Squad**: "ADDS brittle 1 and pinned to the blow. Second upgrade adds exposed."

Each step is a tweak of the drill's turn in its foe (`momentum`, scoped to the Inertia Drill):
a blow in the drill's blow's place, and the foe statuses it lays after it.

## Why it is back

A tier two arrives at level two and picks once, so each of its directions is one step deep (the
validator holds a tier's directions to its picks). At tier two the tree's second steps, Seared and
Exposed, could never be taken.
